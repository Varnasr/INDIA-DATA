Downloads

    This page is in English only. The tables use the names and spellings of the documents they come from.

    Everything at once

    One zip file holds every table on this page as CSV, the published ones and the Chief Electoral Officers' own, with a list of the files and a sheet saying what every column holds.

    One workbook for each place

    Each workbook opens on a sheet saying what every other sheet is, how many rows it has and what it cannot show. Its last sheet says what every column holds. A published table is the one the site's pages draw. A source table is the Chief Electoral Officer's own, as it was transcribed and checked, and is found only in the workbook.

    What each column holds

    Type a column name or a word to find it in every table.

    What is not here

    • No file carries a name, an EPIC number or anything else that identifies an elector. The lists of names the Bihar and Hyderabad counts come from are not published here and will not be.
    • Tables other people compiled from the named lists, which some states' method pages mention, are not used on the site and are not in these files.
    • Election results come from the Election Commission's statistical reports, some through a published compilation of them. They sit inside the state tables only where a page uses them.
    • A state that has not published a table by constituency or district has none here. Each state's method page says what its Chief Electoral Officer has and has not published.
