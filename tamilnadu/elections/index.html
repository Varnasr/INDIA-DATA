Elections

    Names removed against the winning margin

    Each dot is a constituency. It sits across by the names left off the draft roll and up by the 2026 winning margin, the winner's votes less the runner-up's. Dots below the line are seats where more names were removed than the winner won by. Colour shows the party that won. Both axes are on a log scale, so that the many seats with small margins can be told apart.

    How many seats, on each count

    Three ways of setting the removals against the margin, from the widest to the narrowest. The first counts every name left off the draft. The second counts the fall that remains on the final roll. The third leaves out the names marked dead and counts the rest at the seat's own 2026 turnout, the share of its electors who voted.

    Removals by the party that won

    Every seat, by the party that won it

    Each dot is a constituency, placed by the names removed for every 100 electors. Chennai's sixteen seats are in red. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    Names removed per 100 electors in the seats each party won in 2026. The last column sets each seat against the rate of its own district and averages the difference, so that a party's figure is not just a measure of where its seats are. A minus means fewer removals than the district as a whole.
