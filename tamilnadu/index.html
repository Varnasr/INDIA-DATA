Tamil Nadu 2026

Overview

    Find a constituency or district

    Each constituency, the area that elects one member of the state assembly, is shaded by the names left off the draft roll for every 100 electors on the roll before the revision. The draft roll is the roll the revision published once those names had been taken off, and counting per 100 electors lets a small seat be set beside a large one. Tap a constituency on the map or type its name, and its figures appear below with a link you can send.

    Constituency boundaries from DataMeet's assembly constituency map (CC BY 2.5 India), simplified.

    The roll, step by step

    The state's roll, step by step, from before the revision to the final roll of 23 February 2026. After the draft, people could file claims to be added and objections to names on it. The final roll is the draft once those were decided. The bars start above zero so that the changes can be seen, and the figure on each bar is the count.

    Tamil Nadu's dates, as they were set and as they were moved, are drawn beside every other state's on How the revision was run.

    Per 100 electors, by district

    Names left off the draft roll of 19 December 2025, for every 100 electors on the roll before the revision began. Chennai is marked in red.

    The reasons, district by district

    Each district's removals split by the reason entered against the name: dead, shifted or absent, and enrolled in more than one place. Districts are in the same order as above.

    Chennai

    The city's sixteen constituencies, by names removed per 100 electors and by reason.

    Women

    The Chief Electoral Officer, the official in charge of the state's electoral roll, has published no split of the names left off by sex, and no roll by sex from before the revision, so women's share of the removals cannot be counted. What can be counted is the change after the draft. For each district, the grey dot is women's share of the draft roll and the red dot their share of the net rise from the draft to the final roll.

    After the draft

    Each line is a district. Its left end is the names left off the draft roll for every 100 electors on the roll before the revision. Its right end is the fall that still remains on the final roll, after the names added and removed on claims and objections. The flatter the line, the less was made up. Chennai is in red. Hover or tap a line for its district.
