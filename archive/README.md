# Archive: not published

The files here are kept for the record and are not served. Netlify answers 404
for `/archive/*` (see `_redirects`), and the GitHub Pages workflow leaves this
folder out of the artifact it deploys.

`context-reports-2025/` holds the eight Word reports written in September 2025
from the dashboard's first data extract. Their figures come from a booth file
with about three rows per booth and from 2024 party labels that were wrong in
nine seats, so several totals, border figures and alliance comparisons in them
do not hold. The corrected versions are in `reports/`, rebuilt from
`data/bihar/` by `scripts/build_reports.py`, and are the ones to cite.
