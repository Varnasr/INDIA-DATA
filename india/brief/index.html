The SIR in brief

    This page is in English only. The legal section quotes the law in its own words.

    Who this page is for

    It is for anyone who wants to speak or protest about the revision and wants the facts straight first. It sets out what happened, what is documented, what has been reported but not established, and what the figures cannot show. It ends with the rights of anyone who protests. Every point links to its source. Further Reading and Viewing lists the investigations and analyses this page draws on, and sets what commentators have said beside the site's own figures.

    What happened

      What is documented

      Each of these rests on the Election Commission's own orders and letters, the Chief Electoral Officers' notes, or the Supreme Court's orders and judgments.

        Reported in the press

        These come from named press reports and are the outlets' accounts. The Commission's reply sits beside them where one was reported.

          What the figures cannot show

          • They cannot show why any name was removed, or what anyone intended. A reason on a list is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it.
          • They cannot show whether a person who was removed was entitled to stay on the roll. Some of the removed had died or moved, and the lists cannot say how many.
          • They cannot show that the removals decided an election.
          • They say nothing about anyone's citizenship. The Supreme Court held in May 2026 that declining to enrol someone does not declare that person not to be a citizen. The NRC page sets out what the law says.
