The SIR in numbers

    This page is in English only. Every figure on it comes from the state table on the All India page, which links each state to its source.

    Against the winning margins

    A seat's winning margin is the votes between its winner and its runner-up. Where more names left a seat's roll than that margin, those names alone were enough to have decided the seat. Whether they did depends on how those people would have voted, and that can be estimated only where the lists say who they were.
