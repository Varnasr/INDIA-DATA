All India: why names were removed

Every state and union territory in the revision has published how many names it left off the draft roll, and nearly all have said why: the elector was dead, had shifted or could not be found, or was enrolled somewhere else as well. This page puts those figures side by side, state by state. The figures are the Chief Electoral Officers' own, and for Bihar they are counted from the deletion lists. A reason is what an officer entered after a visit or its absence, not a finding.

The reasons, state by state

Each state's removals split by the reason entered, as a share of the removals it has split. States are ordered by the share entered as shifted, absent or other.
