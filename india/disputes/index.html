Protests and disputes

    Next in court

    Hearings and deadlines ahead in the court cases on the revision, soonest first, each with the order or report that gives its date. A date worked out from a period the court gave, such as two weeks, is approximate and is marked so. A date drops off this list once it has passed.

      This page is in English only. Every item quotes the order, record or report it comes from and links to it.

      Search and filter

      Type a word, a name or a number, or narrow the list by state, issue, court, party or body, and place. Every item below follows the choice, and the address keeps it, so a filtered list can be sent as a link.
