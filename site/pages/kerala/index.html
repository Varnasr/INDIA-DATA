Kerala: who was taken off the roll

    From January 2025 to the roll the election was held on

    The state's roll, step by step, from the final roll of the 2025 summary revision to the roll of 23 March 2026. The bars start above zero so that the changes can be seen, and the figure on each bar is the count.

    How much smaller each district's roll was at the draft

    The Chief Electoral Officer has published no count of the names left off the draft, by district or constituency. Every roll is published by constituency, so this is the fall from the roll of January 2025 to the draft of 23 December 2025, for every 100 electors on the January roll. It takes in the ordinary changes of 2025 as well as the revision, though for the state as a whole the two are within half a lakh of each other.

    At the draft and at the election

    For each district, the grey dot is the fall from January 2025 to the draft, and the blue dot the fall to the roll the election was held on, both for every 100 electors on the January roll. A blue dot below zero means the roll was larger at the election than in January 2025. The rise after the draft counts new voters as well as names put back.

    Women

    For each district, the grey dot is women's share of the January 2025 roll and the red dot women's share of the fall to the draft. Where the red dot is to the right, more women left the roll than their share of it.
