    Which rule sent electors to a hearing

    Electors summoned under each rule, by constituency.

    The no-mapping list and the discrepancy list

    Electors summoned, per 100 on the roll before the revision. No mapping means the elector could not be linked to the last intensive revision. The discrepancy list is the Commission's list of mismatches in names, ages and family links. An elector on both lists is counted once, under no mapping.

    Hearings scheduled each day

    Cases listed per day, 28 August to 12 October.

    Load per slot

    A slot is one sitting on the hearing schedule. The table gives the number of slots, the cases in the typical (median) slot, and the cases in the heaviest one.

    Summoned, as a share of the roll

    Electors kept on the draft roll, the roll published after the removals, but called to a hearing.

    "Summoned" counts the Election Commission's discrepancy list and the no-mapping list together. On 7 September the Commission extended the calendar, with claims to 7 October, hearings to 5 November and the final roll on 9 November. The schedules shown here are the ones in the September exports.
