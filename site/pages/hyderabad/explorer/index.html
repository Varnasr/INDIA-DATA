      Left off means on the Absent-Shifted-Dead list and missing from the draft roll, the roll published after the exclusions, on the September 2026 lists. The Commission extended the schedule on 7 September and again on 29 October, as Telangana Today reported, with claims to 7 October, hearings now to 26 November and the final roll on 30 November, so the count will change. Malakpet is shown by polling-station name because its list has no part numbers. "At hearing" counts the Election Commission's discrepancy list and the no-mapping list together. The no-mapping list holds electors who could not be traced to the last intensive revision, and the discrepancy list holds mismatches in names, ages and family links.
