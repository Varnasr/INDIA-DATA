    1. Removals against the 2025 margin

    Names removed in each seat, set against the margin by which the seat was won in 2025. The margin is the winner's votes less the runner-up's. Where more names were removed than that, the removals were large enough to have decided the seat. The lists cannot show whether they did.

    After the final roll

    The draft roll of 1 August 2025 was open to claims, to add a name, and objections, to remove one. The final roll of 30 September 2025 is the result. Here it is set against the roll before the revision. A seat's roll before is its draft roll plus the names on its phase 1 deletion list, because the roll of June 2025 is published only as a state total. The net fall is the roll before less the final roll.

    Each reason for removal, on its own, against the margin

    The phase 1 list gives the officer's reason for every name. That reason is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it. Each reason is taken on its own here. The chart counts the seats where the names removed for that reason alone are more than the 2025 margin, by the alliance that won.

    Could the removals have changed who won?

    How the removed electors might have voted, estimated three ways, and one figure that needs no estimate at all. Each estimate starts from the seat's names still off the final roll, less those entered as dead, and assumes they would have voted at the seat's 2025 turnout. That count is on the low side, because the names added after the draft include new electors. So every estimate is also run on all the names removed, with nothing added back.

    2. Seats, grouped by who held them

    Deletions per 100 electors, with each seat labelled three ways. These are the 2020 Assembly winner, the party that led the seat's segment of the 2024 Lok Sabha vote, and the 2025 Assembly winner.

    Did seats with more removals swing to the NDA?

    If the removals had handed seats to the NDA, then among the seats INDIA held in 2020, those that lost the most names should have gone to the NDA most often in 2025. Here those seats are split into thirds by names removed per 100 electors.

    Did the vote move where more names were removed?

    Between the 2020 and 2025 Assembly elections the NDA parties gained votes across Bihar. If the removals had taken away more of INDIA's voters than the NDA's, INDIA's share should have fallen most where the most names came off the roll. Here every seat is split into thirds by names removed per 100 electors, and both elections are counted with the parties grouped as they stood in 2025.

    3. Booths within the same seat

    Comparing whole seats mixes politics with geography. Inside one constituency every booth has the same registration officer and much the same villages and migration, so comparing booths there takes most of the geography out. Here the booths the NDA led in 2024 are compared with the booths INDIA led, seat by seat.

    NDA-led minus INDIA-led booths, seat by seat

    The average names removed per NDA-led booth, less the average per INDIA-led booth, in each constituency with at least 20 of each. A bar above zero means NDA-led booths lost more.

    5. Seats that changed hands

    The alliance that won each seat in 2020 against the one that won it in 2025, with the deletions per 100 electors in each cell.

    The 2024 booth leaders come from Form 20, the booth-level count, and the top two parties in every Lok Sabha seat match the declared result.     How sure are we?

    A pattern seen across all seats can weaken, or even reverse, once each seat is compared only with seats like it. So each finding here was measured again within each district, within each seat, and with Seemanchal left out.

    The swing, measured six ways

    Each row is one way of asking whether seats that lost more names moved further towards the NDA from 2020 to 2025. The square is the estimate, in points of NDA lead for each extra name removed per 100 electors. The line is its 95% interval, the range the true value most likely lies in. A line that crosses zero cannot rule out no effect. The red row is the comparison that allows for each seat's 2020 lead, which the others leave out.
