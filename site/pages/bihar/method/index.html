    How the lists were prepared

    One count per booth

    The booth list repeats each booth once for each of its three leading parties in 2024, with the same removal count on every row. Counting each booth once gives 77,837 booths and 65,59,626 phase 1 removals, the Chief Electoral Officer's total.

    Party names matched to the declared result

    Votes added up from the booths match the declared 2024 result in every Lok Sabha seat. In nine seats a column of votes carried a party name that differed from the declared result. Those columns were matched to the declared result, and the evidence for each is below. After matching, the first and second parties in all 40 seats are the declared winner and runner-up.

    Titles are not surnames

    Most women carry Devi, Kumari or Khatun in place of a surname, and none of the three says anything about caste. They are set aside, and caste is read first from the surname of the father or husband.

    Reasons are read as entered

    Each name on the phase 1 lists has the reason the officer gave for removing it. The four reasons are counted as written and none is regrouped. The counts agree with the booth list in all 77,837 booths, and the counts of women and men agree with the Chief Electoral Officer's phase 1 figures in every constituency. The phase 2 lists give no reasons.

    Constituencies are joined by number

    Bihar has two seats called Kalyanpur (16 and 131) and two called Pipra (17 and 42), so every match between tables uses the constituency number. Minapur (90) belongs to the Vaishali Lok Sabha seat.

    Border constituencies

    A constituency counts as a border constituency when its district touches Nepal, West Bengal, Jharkhand or Uttar Pradesh.

    Research questions

    What this shows and what it does not

    The lists show how many names were removed and from which booths, and the sex and name of each person removed, which is enough to set the removals against the margins that decided each seat. They also record the reason the officer gave for each phase 1 removal. They cannot show whether that reason was true, whether the removal was right, or how the elector voted. Religion and caste are guessed from names. Questions and answers.

    Removed means named on the SIR deletion lists. Per 100 electors is removals across both phases divided by the constituency's 2025 electorate. The booth leader is the party with the most votes in the booth in 2024, and the segment leader the party with the most votes in the assembly segment in 2024.
