If your name was left off

    This page is in English only for now, because the steps follow the wording of the law and the orders. Every step links to the document it rests on.

    This page sets out what the law and the Election Commission's own orders give an elector whose name was left off the draft or the final roll, and where to get help. It is general information drawn from those documents, and it is not legal advice on any one case. The state's Chief Electoral Officer, the registration officer and the District Legal Services Authority can say how a step works in a particular state.

    Organisations that have helped electors

    Listing an organisation here is not an endorsement, and leaving one out says nothing about it. If you know of an organisation that helped electors, or a listing is out of date, write to the address at the foot of the page with a link that shows it.
