Sources and method

    This page is in English only for now. Every figure on it is the Election Commission's or the Chief Electoral Officer's own.

    What is counted

    The state figures

    Three rolls matter here. The roll before the revision () is the list when the revision began. The draft roll is the list published once the enumeration forms were collected, and it leaves off anyone whose form did not come back. The final roll is the draft after the names added and deleted on claims and objections. The roll before, the forms collected and the three reasons are from the press note of 16 December 2025 by the Chief Electoral Officer (CEO), who keeps the state's rolls. It gives the reasons only in lakh (one lakh is 100,000), rounded. The draft roll, the names added and deleted after it and the final roll, by sex, are from the CEO's fact sheet of 21 February 2026. All of them cover the 199 constituencies revised together. Anta was revised later, after its bye-election of November 2025, and is outside them. The names left off the draft, , are the roll before less the draft roll. No document prints that number. The three rounded reasons add up to lakh.

    Why the change in the roll below the state

    No table of the names left off has been published by assembly constituency (AC) or district. The only source below the state is the lists of individual electors, which this site does not open. So the constituency and district pages compare two published rolls of general electors. One is the roll the 2023 assembly election was held on, from the Election Commission's statistical report (AC-wise number of electors). The other is the final SIR roll of 12 March 2026, from the CEO's district and constituency table. General electors are everyone on the roll except service voters, who are left out of both because they cluster in a few districts and would distort the change there. The change takes in more than two years of new voters, deaths and moves as well as the revision, so it is never called the removals. For the state it is a fall of , against left off the draft.

    Women

    The 2026 table counts 565 third-gender electors among women, statewide, with no split by constituency. To compare like with like, the 2023 third-gender electors are added to women too. No split by sex of the names left off the draft has been published.

    The 2023 result

    Winner, runner-up and winning margin (the winner's votes less the runner-up's) for every seat are from the Election Commission's statistical report for the 2023 election, including Karanpur, whose poll was held in January 2024. Seats that changed hands at later bye-elections, Anta among them, are not reflected.

    Checks

    The site is not built unless every check passes. Every table must have each of the 200 constituencies once. The 2023 general electors must add up to the Election Commission's total, and the 2026 constituencies to the fact sheet's final roll. The draft plus additions less deletions must equal the final roll. Every margin must equal the winner's votes less the runner-up's.

    Against the district

    Some districts' rolls shrank far more than others, and the parties did not win evenly across districts. A party that won many seats in a district whose roll fell a lot would then show a large fall in its seats, and the cause would be the district. So each seat's change per 100 electors is also set against its own district's, and those differences are averaged over each party's seats.

    What these tables cannot show

    • How many names were removed in each district or constituency, or on what recorded reason.
    • How many of those left off were put back.
    • Whether each removal was right, or how anyone missing from the roll would have voted.
