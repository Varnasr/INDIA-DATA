Sources and method

    This page is in English only for now. Every figure on it is the Election Commission's or the Chief Electoral Officer's own.

    What is counted

    The state figures

    The roll before the revision (), the forms collected and the three reasons are from the Chief Electoral Officer's press note of 16 December 2025, which gives the reasons only in lakh, rounded. The draft roll, the names added and deleted after it and the final roll, by sex, are from the Chief Electoral Officer's fact sheet of 21 February 2026. All of them cover the 199 constituencies revised together. Anta was revised later, after its bye-election of November 2025, and is outside them. The names left off the draft, , are the roll before less the draft roll. No document prints that number, and the three rounded reasons add up to lakh.

    Why the change in the roll below the state

    No table of the names left off has been published by constituency or district. The only source below the state is the lists of individual electors, which this site does not open. So the constituency and district pages compare two published rolls of general electors: the roll the 2023 assembly election was held on, from the Election Commission's statistical report (AC-wise number of electors), and the final SIR roll of 12 March 2026, from the Chief Electoral Officer's district and constituency table. Service voters are left out of both. The change takes in more than two years of new voters, deaths and moves as well as the revision, so it is never called the removals. For the state it is a fall of , against left off the draft.

    Women

    The 2026 table counts 565 third-gender electors among women, statewide, without a split by constituency. The 2023 third-gender electors are therefore added to women before the two years are compared. No split by sex of the names left off the draft has been published.

    The 2023 result

    Winner, runner-up and margin for every seat are from the Election Commission's statistical report for the 2023 election, including Karanpur, whose poll was held in January 2024. Seats that changed hands at later bye-elections, Anta among them, are not reflected.

    Checks

    The build fails unless every table has each of the 200 constituencies once, the 2023 general electors add up to the Election Commission's total, the 2026 constituencies add up to the fact sheet's final roll, the draft plus additions less deletions equals the final roll, and every margin equals the winner's votes less the runner-up's.

    Against the district

    Some districts' rolls shrank far more than others, and the parties did not win evenly across districts. So each seat's change per 100 is also set against its own district's, and those differences are averaged for each party's seats.

    What these tables cannot show

    • How many names were removed in each district or constituency, or on what recorded reason.
    • How many of those left off were put back.
    • Whether each removal was right, or how anyone missing from the roll would have voted.
