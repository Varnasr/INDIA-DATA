    The reasons

    The reasons were published for the state only, in lakh. A reason is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it.

    Per 100 electors, by district

    No count of the names left off has been published below the state. Every roll is published by constituency, so each dot here is one constituency's change in its roll of general electors, from the roll the 2023 election was held on to the final roll of 12 March 2026, for every 100 electors on the 2023 roll, grouped by district. It takes in more than two years of ordinary changes, new voters among them, as well as the revision, so it is a different count from the names left off. Below zero, the roll grew. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    Women

    Each dot is a constituency. Across, women's share of its 2023 roll; up, women's share of its final roll in 2026. Below the dashed line, women are a smaller share of the roll than they were. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    Against the 2023 margins

    Each dot is a constituency. Across, the margin its 2023 winner won by; up, the fall in its roll from 2023 to the final roll. Above the dashed line, the roll fell by more than the margin. Below zero, the roll grew.
