    The fall in the roll against the winning margin

    Each dot is a constituency. It sits across by how much smaller its roll was at the 2026 nominations than at the 2024 Lok Sabha nominations, and up by the 2026 winning margin, the winner's votes less the runner-up's. The fall in the roll is a different count from the names left off the draft. Dots below the line are seats where the roll fell by more than the winner won by. Colour shows the party that won.

    How many seats, on each count

    Three ways of setting the fall in the roll against the margin. The first stops at the draft roll of December 2025. The second runs to the roll the election was held on, after the names held back for adjudication. The third counts the second at the seat's own 2026 turnout, the share of its electors who voted. None of them separates the names entered as dead, because the reasons are published for the state only.
