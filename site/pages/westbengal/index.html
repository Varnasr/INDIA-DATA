West Bengal: who was taken off the roll

    From the roll before the revision to the roll the election was held on

    The state's roll, step by step, as the Chief Electoral Officer reported it. The names left off the draft by reason, the claims and objections, the names held back for adjudication, and the change between the final roll and the roll at the 2026 nominations. The bars start above zero so that the changes can be seen, and the figure on each bar is the count.

    How much smaller each district's roll was

    The Chief Electoral Officer has published no count of the names removed by district or constituency. What is published for every constituency is the roll at the 2024 Lok Sabha nominations and the roll at the 2026 Assembly nominations. This is the fall between the two, for every 100 electors on the 2024 roll. It takes in eighteen months of ordinary additions and deletions as well as the revision. Kolkata's two districts are marked in red.

    Before and after the draft

    For each district, the grey dot is the fall from the 2024 roll to the draft roll of December 2025, and the blue dot the fall to the roll the election was held on, both for every 100 electors on the 2024 roll. The gap between them is what happened after the draft, mostly the names held back for adjudication.

    Women

    For each district, the grey dot is women's share of the 2024 roll and the red dot women's share of the fall in the roll. Where the red dot is to the right, more women left the roll than their share of it.
