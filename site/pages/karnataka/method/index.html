Method

    What is counted

    Names left off the draft roll

    The Chief Electoral Officer, Karnataka, published the draft roll on 24 August 2026 with a table of the names left off it, by election district and under five reasons, which are absent, permanently shifted, dead, already enrolled and other. The 34 district rows add up to the state row, names out of , and in every district the names left off and the enumeration forms digitised add up to the roll of 16 June 2026. An enumeration form is the form each elector on the roll had to return to stay on it.

    Why districts and not constituencies

    The Chief Electoral Officer published every table of the revision by its 34 election districts and for the state, and none by assembly constituency. Bengaluru appears as four of those districts, which are Bangalore Urban and the North, Central and South districts of the city corporation, B.B.M.P. A count by constituency could be made only by tallying the named lists, which this site does not do in Karnataka.

    The roll after the draft

    The final roll is due on 27 October 2026. Until then the latest figures are the roll of 9 October 2026, by district and sex, published after claims and objections had closed and while notices were still being heard. The rise from the draft to that roll is the names added less those deleted since, so it counts new voters as well as names put back, and it is the most that could have been restored.

    Women and men

    The draft roll and the roll of 9 October are published by sex. The roll of 16 June is published as a total only, and the names left off are not split by sex, so the share of women among those left off cannot be counted from the Chief Electoral Officer's tables.

    The map

    The Chief Electoral Officer's 34 election districts are not the state's revenue districts, so no district map draws them. The map draws each district as the constituencies the Election Commission's own list places in it, from DataMeet's assembly constituency map (CC BY 2.5 India). Bengaluru's three B.B.M.P districts are drawn that way, and Harapanahalli sits in Vijayanagara.

    Checks

    All three tables have the same 34 districts, each district's reasons add up to its names left off, each district's draft roll equals its roll before less its names left off, men, women and third gender add up, and the state totals equal the figures in the Chief Electoral Officer's press notes.

    What these tables cannot show

    • Which constituencies lost the most names, or how the names left off compare with the margins of the 2023 election.
    • Whether women, the old or the young were left off more often.
    • Whether each name was rightly left off. A reason on the list is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it.
