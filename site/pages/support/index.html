This page is in English only.

Off the Roll is researched, checked and kept up to date by a small team at PinPoint Ventures. Every figure is free to read and to download. If you would like to support the work, fill in this form and we will write back.

How it works

  • Tell us who you are and how you would like to help. A contribution of money is one way. Data, research, translation and spreading the word are others.
  • The page that opens after you send the form has a UPI QR code for a contribution and a button that opens your UPI app. We also write back to the email you give.
  • Support does not buy influence over what the site publishes. Every figure still comes from a source, and the methods stay as they are.

What you type here goes to Netlify, which hosts this site and keeps it for us, and is used only to write back to you. It is never published and never shared. The terms and privacy page says more.
