ECINET, in date order

    This page is in English only. Every item quotes the report it comes from and links to it.

    What ECINET is

    ECINET is the Election Commission's platform for its election officials and other users. The Commission announced it in May 2025 as one platform bringing together more than 40 of its apps and websites, among them ERONET, and launched it on 22 January 2026, saying a trial version had been used in Bihar's revision. The revision's enumeration forms and its notice and hearing dashboard are on it. This page gathers every item on the disputes page that concerns ECINET or ERONET, whatever section it sits in, so the record can be read in order.

      What this record cannot show

      The review of ECINET's code read only what the site serves before login, on the dates it gives, and cannot say whether any control it found was used, by whom or how often. Nothing here says who designed a feature or why. The Commission's own statements are listed beside the reports wherever they were made.
