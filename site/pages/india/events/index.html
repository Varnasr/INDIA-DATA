        What is listed here

        Each event gives the line from its source that states the date. An event whose date was found in a single news report says so. Organisations that hold hearings or meetings on the revision can send us their announcements, and we list them once we have read the announcement itself.

        The Election Commission's own camps, deadlines for claims and objections, and the dates of the final rolls are on the Rules page. Court hearings are in the courts section of the disputes page.
