Logical discrepancies

    After the draft roll, an elector who had been linked to the roll of the last intensive revision could still be sent a notice if the link showed a mismatch in names, ages or family ties. The Commission calls these logical discrepancies. Below are the rules as a Chief Electoral Officer, the Supreme Court and the press reported them, and a check that lists which of them match an entry.
