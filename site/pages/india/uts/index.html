Territories

    This page is in English only. Every figure on it comes from the All India table, which links each figure to its source, and from each territory's own page.

    Names left off, per 100 electors

    India has eight union territories. Six of them were in the revision. Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh were left out of the Commission's order, which said their dates would be announced later. The chart sets each territory's names left off the draft against its roll before the revision, beside the rate for every state and territory together.

    The reasons

    The share of each territory's names left off under each of the three reasons every state reported. A reason is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it.

    After the draft

    The net change from the draft to the final roll, for every 100 names the draft left off. It counts people enrolling for the first time as well as names put back, so it is the most that could have been made up. Below zero, the final roll is smaller than the draft.
