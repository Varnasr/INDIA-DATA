    Why these places

    Each of these places stands out in its own state's figures. Kichha lost the largest share of its roll of any constituency in Uttarakhand, and Tughlakabad the largest in Delhi. Danilimda is the one Ahmedabad seat whose roll shrank again after the draft. Murshidabad and Malda had the most names sent for adjudication in West Bengal. Bhiwandi East, Anna Nagar, Lucknow and B.B.M.P (South) lost the largest share of their roll at the draft in Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka. Lakhimpur's roll fell further after the draft than any other seat's in Assam's Special Revision. A figure alone cannot say why. So for each place this page sets out its figures, then what the official records say, what was reported, what officials and parties said, and what could not be found. The states measure different things, so the places are never compared with each other.

    Items that named or identified a private person, rested on anyone's religion, caste or citizenship, or came from an analysis of the named lists were left out, as they are everywhere else. Find any constituency.
