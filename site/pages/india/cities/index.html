The cities

    This page is in English only. Every figure on it comes from the state pages, which link each table to its source.

    Each city against its state

    Names left off the draft roll for every 100 electors on the roll before the revision, in the city and in its state as a whole. A city here is the districts named in the last section, because no state published its figures by city boundary.

    The city and its state, per 100 electors

    The core, the suburbs and the rest of the state

    The core is the city's own districts. The suburbs are the neighbouring districts its growth has spread into. The rest of the state is everything else. Delhi's suburbs on this page are Ghaziabad and Gautam Buddha Nagar, measured on Uttar Pradesh's revision. Gurugram and Faridabad are in Haryana, which this site has no district figures for.

    What was written against the names

    Where a state published the reasons by district, the share of the city's names left off that were entered as shifted or absent, and the share entered as dead. A reason is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it.

    The lists cannot say which of two very different things a high "shifted or absent" share means. It can mean people who had moved and were also registered somewhere else, such as a home village, and were rightly taken off one roll. It can equally mean people who still live in the city and were not at home when the officer called. The count is the same either way, so this page does not say which it is.

    Kolkata and Kochi

    West Bengal and Kerala published no names left off below the state. What they published by district is the roll itself, so for these two cities the measure is the fall in the roll: West Bengal's from the 2024 Lok Sabha roll to the draft, Kerala's from the roll of January 2025 to the draft. That fall takes in ordinary deaths and moves over more than a year as well as the revision, so it is a different count from the others on this page and is never ranked with them.
