The roll and the 2026 election

    The fall at the draft against the winning margin

    Each dot is a constituency. Across, how much smaller its roll was at the draft of December 2025 than in January 2025; up, the 2026 winning margin in votes. Dots below the line are seats where the roll fell by more than the winner won by. Colour shows the party that won.

    How many seats, on each count

    Three ways of setting the fall in the roll against the margin. The first stops at the draft roll. The second runs to the roll the election was held on, after the names added on claims and before the poll. The third counts the second at the seat's own 2026 turnout.

    The fall by the party that won

    Every seat, by the party that won it

    Each dot is a constituency, placed by how much its roll fell at the draft for every 100 electors on the January 2025 roll. Thiruvananthapuram's seats are in red. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    The fall at the draft per 100 electors in the seats each party won in 2026. The last column sets each seat against its own district and averages the difference, so that a party is not credited with a district simply for winning there.
