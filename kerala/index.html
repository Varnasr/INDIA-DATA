Kerala 2026

Figures as of 21 February 2026, when the final roll was published.

Overview

    Find a constituency or district

    Each constituency, the area that elects one member of the state assembly, is shaded by how much smaller its roll was at the draft than in January 2025, for every 100 electors on the January roll. The draft roll is the roll the revision published once names had been taken off. This fall in the roll is a different count from the names removed, as the district section below explains. Tap a constituency on the map or type its name, and its figures appear below with a link you can send.

    Constituency boundaries from DataMeet's assembly constituency map (CC BY 2.5 India), simplified.

    The roll, step by step

    The state's roll, step by step, from the final roll of the 2025 summary revision, the ordinary yearly update, to the roll of 23 March 2026. After the draft, people could file claims to be added and objections to names on it. The final roll is the draft once those were decided. The bars start above zero so that the changes can be seen, and the figure on each bar is the count.

    Kerala's dates, as they were set and as they were moved, are drawn beside every other state's on How the revision was run.

    Per 100 electors, by district

    The Chief Electoral Officer, the official in charge of the state's electoral roll, has published no count of the names left off the draft, by district or constituency. Every roll is published by constituency, so each dot here is one constituency's fall from the roll of January 2025 to the draft of 23 December 2025, for every 100 electors on the January roll, grouped by district. It takes in the ordinary changes of 2025 as well as the revision, though for the state as a whole the two are within half a lakh of each other. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    Women

    Each dot is a constituency. It sits across by women's share of its January 2025 roll and up by women's share of the fall in its roll at the draft. Dots above the dashed line are seats where women were a larger share of the fall than of the roll. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    After the draft

    Each small chart follows one district's roll through the four published rolls, January 2025, the draft, the final roll and March 2026, with January 2025 set at 100. The dashed line is the January level. Thiruvananthapuram is in red. The rise after the draft counts new voters as well as names put back.
