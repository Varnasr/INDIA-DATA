Uttarakhand: who was taken off the roll

    This page is in English only for now. Every figure on it comes from the Election Commission or the Chief Electoral Officer, and where it reached us through the press, the page says so.

    Find a constituency or district

    An assembly constituency is the area that elects one member of the state assembly, and Uttarakhand has 70. Type a constituency's name or number, or a district, and its figures appear below with a link you can send.

    Each constituency is shaded by the names removed from it after the draft, for every 1,000 electors on its draft roll. Tap a constituency for its figures. Boundaries drawn from DataMeet's assembly constituency map (CC BY 2.5 India), simplified.

    The roll, step by step

    The roll before the revision is the list of electors on 8 June 2026, when the revision began. The draft roll of 14 July 2026 is the list published once the enumeration forms were collected, and a name whose form did not come back was left off it. Electors could then ask to be added, or object to names, until 13 August, and the final roll came out on 3 October 2026. The bars start above zero so that the changes can be seen.

    Uttarakhand's dates, as they were set and as they were moved, are drawn beside every other state's on How the revision was run.

    The reasons

    Each name left off the draft carries one of four reasons. The reasons were published for Uttarakhand as a whole, and the chart counts them in lakh (one lakh is 100,000). A reason is what the officer wrote down, and nobody checked it.

    After the draft, constituency by constituency

    After the draft, a name is added when a claim is admitted (Form 6, or Form 8 for someone moving in) and removed when an objection is admitted (Form 7, or Form 8 for someone moving out). Each dot is a constituency. Its place across is the names added for every 1,000 on its draft roll, and its height the names removed. Above the dashed line, more were removed than added, so the final roll is smaller than the draft.

    The plains and the hills

    Four districts take in the plains of the state, Dehradun, Haridwar, Nainital and Udham Singh Nagar, and the other nine are in the hills. Dehradun and Nainital also take in hill constituencies, so this is a split by district. The chart shows the plains districts' share of the draft roll, of the names added after it and of the names removed after it.

    Per 1,000 electors, by district

    Each dot is one constituency's names removed after the draft, for every 1,000 electors on its draft roll, grouped by district. Hover or tap a dot for its name.

    Women

    Each dot is a constituency. Its place across is women's share of its draft roll, and its height their share of the names removed from it after the draft. Above the dashed line, women were a larger share of those removed than of the roll. The table gives the count by sex and no reason for any removal.
