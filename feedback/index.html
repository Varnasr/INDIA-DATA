Feedback and corrections

This page is in English only. You can write to us in any language.

Tell us about a figure that looks wrong, something that is hard to follow, a page that does not work, or anything you would like the site to show. Every message is read.

What happens next

  • A report of a wrong figure is checked against the source the figure comes from. If the site is wrong, the page is corrected and the change is listed on the corrections page with what the page said and what it says now.
  • If you give an email, we write back. A report about a figure is acknowledged within 24 hours and answered within 15 days, as the terms say.
  • A suggestion is weighed against what the published records can show. The site adds a figure only when it has a source that can be checked.
  • Your name and email are optional and are never published. Please do not send the name or voter ID number of anyone on a roll.

What you type here is kept privately for us and is used only to answer you and to correct the site. It is never published and never shared. The terms and privacy page says more.
