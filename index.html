Bihar 2024 – Deletions, Demography & Electoral Patterns Working analysis

Assembly-level and booth-level deletions from Bihar’s electoral rolls, linked to district-level demographics, caste groups, religion and rural–urban profile, to explore possible political patterns. This is designed for exploratory use, not as a definitive causal statement.
Data sources: Bihar deletions dataset (Form 20), Bihar Caste Survey 2023, district profiles (population, GDP, rural–urban), electoral outcomes 2020 & 2024.
