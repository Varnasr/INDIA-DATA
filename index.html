Reading these visuals with caution
This tab is meant for pattern-finding, not deterministic claims. For example, a higher average EBC share in one alliance’s ACs does not by itself imply “EBC vote banks” – that would require turnout and vote share analysis layered on top of this structural picture.

Methods, data sources, and cautions

A compact record of what each CSV represents, and how it has been used here. You can expand or edit this text to match your final paper.

Change log: manual
Core deletions and AC summaries
  • overview_metrics.csv – total deletions, number of booths, female share of deletions, and share of high-deletion booths.
  • elections_summary_working.csv – one row per AC with alliance labels for 2020 VS and 2024 LS, number of booths, deletions, and deletions per booth.
  • elections_quartiles_2024_by_deletion.csv – quartile labels, alliance win rates by quartile, and the number of ACs in each band.

All AC-level deletions are based on the cleaned roll data, not on turnout or votes cast.

Religion, gender, and booth-level structure
  • religion_overall.csv – overall counts and percentage shares of deletions by broad religious category.
  • religion_gender_crosstab.csv – deletions by religion and gender, with row-wise gender shares.
  • religion_by_band.csv – religious composition within low, medium, and high deletion bands (by booth count).
  • booth-gender-religion.csv – raw booth-level counts of deletions by gender and religion, used here only in aggregated form.

Bands and thresholds are defined upstream in the data preparation scripts; this dashboard does not recompute them in the browser.

Caste survey mapping and reservation
  • bcs_ac_shares_estimated.csv – estimated caste category shares (EBC, OBC, SC, ST, “forward”) for each AC, mapped from the Bihar caste survey.
  • ac_reserved_from_name.csv – reservation type by AC (GEN / SC / ST / others), parsed from the constituency naming / ECI documentation.

All caste measures are structural shares at AC level. Inferring actual political behaviour from these requires further modelling and, ideally, survey or booth-level voting data.

Key cautions for interpretation
  • These are deletions from the electoral roll, not vote counts or turnout levels.
  • Alliance labels are simplified to NDA / INDIA / OTHER for descriptive clarity.
  • The Bihar caste survey provides compositional information; it does not encode partisan preference.
  • High deletion quartiles are flags for further investigation, not proof of manipulation on their own.

Treat this dashboard as the descriptive front-end to a deeper paper. Any strong causal claims should be made only in that fuller analytical context.
