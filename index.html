The Special Intensive Revision: who was struck off the rolls

In 2025 the Election Commission began re-verifying every elector, state by state, instead of revising the existing rolls. These pages follow two of those revisions through the Commission's own lists: Bihar, the first state to go through it, and five constituencies of Hyderabad's Old City.

      What an audit needs

      Neither set of lists says whose votes the removals were. Three things would settle it.

      • 1A name-by-name verification of removals in every Bihar constituency where they exceed the 2025 winning margin, beginning with the seats that changed hands.
      • 2Publication, booth by booth, of every claim and objection filed after the draft roll and what became of it.
      • 3In Hyderabad, a review before the final roll of every removal entered as "untraceable or absent", and of removals of electors with no link to the 2002 roll.

      Two kinds of list

      Bihar's lists name the removed, booth by booth, so the Bihar pages compare where removals fell and who the removed were against census and survey baselines. Hyderabad's lists cover every elector on the pre-revision roll, so each group there has its own removal rate, and groups can be compared inside the same polling part.
