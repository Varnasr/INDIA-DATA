Ranchi: Names of 43,61,987 voters have been removed from the draft voters  list published by the Election Commission of India (ECI) on Wednesday following the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) exercise in Jharkhand, downsizing the number of electors from 2,64,63,236 to 2,21,01,249.Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) K Ravi Kumar said voters whose names were removed fall under the Absent, Shifted, Dead, or Duplicate (ASDD) category.As per statistics, 4,38,596 voters were listed in multiple locations, while 7,63,903 were placed into the  dead  category. Additionally, 14,50,810 electors were not found at the addresses recorded in the voter list, and 1,16,484 voters were identified as foreign nationals or falling under other categories.The ECI said the period for filing claims and objections on the draft roll has been fixed from August 5 to September 4. If a name is missing from the draft roll, the prescribed form needs to be submitted to the ECI.The CEO said youths turning 18 can fill out Form 6 to enrol their names in the voter list.  Since no new voters have been registered in Jharkhand since October 1, 2024, all youths turning 18 between October 1, 2024, and October 1, 2026, are eligible to enrol as electors,  Kumar said.He added that extensive arrangements have been made at higher education institutions to facilitate the new voter registration process.Following a reorganisation, the number of polling stations has increased in all assembly constituencies, except for Kolebira, taking the final count from 29,571 to 31,892.
