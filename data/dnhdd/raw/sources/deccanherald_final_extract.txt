<p>New Delhi: Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu has recorded a 30.88 per cent drop in voters in the final electoral rolls published after <a href=\"https://www.deccanherald.com/tags/special-intensive-revision\">Special Intensive Revision (SIR)</a>, the highest for any of the four state or union territory that has unveiled the new voter lists.</p><p>The union territory now has 2,95,833 voters in its list, published on September 11, as against the pre-SIR count of 4,28,036. The draft list had 3,01,142 voters and the UT has seen a further drop in its list.</p><p>The gender ratio is 1,029 women for every 1,000 men voters. According to the statistics, there are 1,45,809 male voters and 1,50,024 female voters while no transgender voter has their name in the list.</p><p>Among the states and UTs that have published the final voter list, Sikkim is the only other state that has recorded a drop in number of voters compared to draft lists while Manipur and Mizoram have recorded an increase.</p>.SIR: Massive voter deletions in Hyderabad, Secunderabad raise 2029 poll stakes.<p>If one takes all the four states and UTs for which final lists are out, there are 35,10,789 voters in the new lists as against 38,67,198 voters in the pre-SIR list, registering a 9.21 per cent drop in voters. There were 34,97,741 voters in the draft lists prepared during the SIR exercise.</p><p>According to the electoral rolls, Manipur saw the highest deletions of 1,32,456 voters from the pre-SIR list while Sikkim saw 52,551 and Mizoram 39,199. </p><p>The Manipur list has declined by 6.32 per cent -- from 20,93,076 as in May this year before kickstarting SIR to 19,60,620 now. The Mizoram list shows a decrease of 4.47 per cent or 39,199 from 8,75,068 to 8,35,869. </p><p>Sikkim voter list was down by 11.15 per cent or 52,551 -- from 4,71,018 to 4,18,467. </p><p><strong>SIR: Roll call</strong></p><p>Pre-SIR /Draft Rolls /Final Rolls /PreSIR-Final / per cent of Decline</p><p>Dadra Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu -- 4,28,036 / 3,01,142 / 2,95,833 / 1,32,203 / 30.88 per cent</p><p>Total ( all 4 states and UTs where final list is out) -- 38,67,198 / 34,97,741 / 35,10,789 / 3,56,409 / 9.21 per cent</p>
