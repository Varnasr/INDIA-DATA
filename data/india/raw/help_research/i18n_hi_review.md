## Changes

- "Held camps in Muslim-majority areas during enumeration to explain the forms and documents, about 500 a day by its own count."
  Problem: "रोज़ लगभग 500" did not say what the 500 counted. The source gives about 500 camps a day.
  Fix: "... अपनी गिनती के अनुसार रोज़ लगभग 500 शिविर।"

- "Ask to be put back on the roll with Form 6"
  Problem: "नाम वापस जुड़वाएँ" reads as "get your name put back", which promises the outcome. The English says only that you ask for it.
  Fix: "फ़ॉर्म 6 भरकर मतदाता सूची में नाम वापस जोड़ने का आवेदन करें"

## Doubtful, left unchanged

- "Shown by" is translated "स्रोत" (source). This is fine as a label beside a report link, but it is less exact than the English.
- Some proper names stay in Latin script: Cochin Diocese, Mahallu, Vallakkadavu, the Coimbatore localities, PUCL Chhattisgarh, National Grievance Service Portal and Book-a-Call with BLO. This looks deliberate, since the names match the source and the app, but a native reader may prefer them in Devanagari.
- "progeny mapping" is transliterated as "प्रोजेनी मैपिंग". It matches the SIR term, but a reader may not know it.
