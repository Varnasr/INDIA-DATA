# Second reading of 31 October 2026: Tripura put back again, and Jharkhand's claims date

Every source below was read in full on 31 October 2026 with curl, from the
JSON-LD articleBody where the page carries one and from the page text where it
does not. Times are as the pages print them.

## Tripura

- Priyanka Deb Barman in Hindustan Times, 30 October 2026, 2:21 pm IST.
  Quotes the letter of Under Secretary Sandeep Kumar to the Chief Electoral
  Officer: "I am directed to refer to your letter dated 19.10.2026 requesting
  therein deferment of the schedule of Special Intensive Revision ( SIR) in the
  State of Tripura in view of the General Elections to Urban Local Bodies".
  The revised schedule was announced on Thursday, 29 October. Preparation,
  training and printing 15 to 24 December, house visits 26 December to 25
  January, draft 1 February, claims and objections 1 February to 2 March,
  disposal 1 February to 1 April, final roll 7 April. "Earlier, the qualifying
  date for SIR in Tripura was fixed on October 1". It says the elections are
  to be held in November and gives no number of bodies.
- IANS in The Hans India, 30 October 2026, 7:26 am IST. Elections to 21 urban
  local bodies including the Agartala Municipal Corporation, the earlier move
  for the Village Committee elections of 28 September under the TTAADC, and a
  senior official of the Election Department: "with January 1, 2027, as the
  qualifying date". House visits 26 December to 25 January 2027, draft 1
  February 2027, final 7 April 2027.
- Sentinel Digital Desk in The Sentinel, 31 October 2026, 10:55 am IST. The
  same dates, with the notice phase and disposal from 1 February to 1 April
  2027 and rationalisation of polling stations by 27 January 2027.

### Verdict

Confirmed. The timeline entry, the schedule step and the reported date for 7
April agree with all three. The qualifying date is not a conflict between the
sources. Hindustan Times gives the earlier one, 1 October, and the official
IANS quotes gives the one for the deferred revision, 1 January 2027, and the
entry already says it that way. No source gives a qualifying date of 1
October for the new schedule. The number of urban local bodies, 21, is IANS's
alone. The step's label calls the Commission's communication a letter of
29.10.2026. Hindustan Times calls it a notification and gives the day it was
announced, not the date printed on it.

Two corrections.

- The News on AIR entry of 10 September (draft 12 December, final 15 February
  2027) had been replaced by the Hindustan Times entry in reported_dates. It
  is restored ahead of the new one, so the history of Tripura's final-roll
  dates on the process page keeps the move to 15 February. The latest entry
  is still the one every page reads.
- raw/states.csv still said Tripura's draft was rescheduled to 12 December
  2026, from News on AIR. It now says 1 February 2027, from Hindustan Times.

## Jharkhand: claims closed on 31 October, and 23 November is the disposal date

reported_dates carried Jharkhand's `claims_end` as 23 November 2026, with Town
Post as its source. Town Post (Anil Tiwary, 8 October 2026, 9:07 pm IST) gives
only the final roll on 30 November, from the Chief Electoral Officer's post on
X, and says "the post does not say whether any other dates in the schedule have
changed". It gives no claims date. The 23 November came from no source the
entry names.

The revised schedule the Chief Electoral Officer, K. Ravi Kumar, issued to the
districts on 8 October gives these dates.

- Vivek Chandra in Prabhat Khabar, Ranchi, 8 October 2026, 4:15 pm IST.
  Notices decided by 22 October. Digitisation of all claims and objections on
  Forms 6, 6A, 7 and 8 by 31 October. Home-to-roll verification of eligible
  people left out, and digitisation of their Form 6, from 1 to 12 November.
  "सभी प्राप्त दावों और आपत्तियों का अंतिम निष्पादन 23 नवंबर तक पूरा करना है."
  Checks and printing 24 to 29 November, final roll 30 November. Qualifying
  date 1 October 2026.
- Chandan Kumar in Prabhat Khabar, Khunti, 10 October 2026, 8:54 am IST. The
  same table, with "23 नवंबर: दावों और आपत्तियों का निस्तारण पूरा करना".
- Vivek Chandra in Prabhat Khabar, Ranchi, 10 October 2026, 11:08 am IST:
  "निर्वाचन आयोग ने दावा-आपत्ति की अंतिम तिथि 31 अक्टूबर निर्धारित की है."
- ETV Bharat Jharkhand, 8 October 2026, 6:47 pm IST. "अब दावा-आपत्ति पर
  सुनवाई 23 नवंबर तक होगी". Electors who could not submit a form can submit
  Form 6 during the home-to-roll verification of 1 to 12 November.
- 22Scope (Desk Jharkhand), 24 October 2026, 1:42 pm IST. Forms 6, 6A, 7 and 8
  to be submitted and digitised by 31 October 2026, and all claims and
  objections finally disposed of by 23 November 2026.

So claims and objections closed on 31 October 2026, and 23 November is the
date by which they are to be heard and decided. A web search on 31 October
found no later extension of the claims date.

### Correction

- The Town Post entry keeps the final roll on 30 November and its quote, with
  `claims_end` set to null.
- A new entry from Prabhat Khabar of 10 October gives the final roll on 30
  November and `claims_end` 31 October 2026, with the quote above. It is the
  latest entry for Jharkhand, so the claims box (assets/sir.js, `stage()`)
  reads 31 October from it and the line under each page reads the final roll
  from it. reported_dates has no field for a disposal date, so 23 November is
  recorded here and not drawn.

### For a corrections entry

From 9 October 2026, when `claims_end` was added, to this correction, the
claims box on Jharkhand's pages and on the help page told Jharkhand's electors
that claims and objections were open until 23 November. They closed on 31
October. The box is drawn by script from process.json, so no built page
carries the sentence, but every reader of those pages was shown it. Until
today the box was right that the window was open. From 1 November it would
have been wrong.

One thing left for the next change. After 31 October the box puts Jharkhand in
its closed state, and that state reads the notice period from the latest
schedule step, which is the order of 14 May with notices decided by 3 October.
The revised schedule moved that to 22 October for notices and 23 November for
all claims and objections, and neither date is in a schedule step. A step from
the revised schedule of 8 October, from Prabhat Khabar's table, would give the
box the right date.

Added after the second reading: a Jharkhand schedule step from the Chief Electoral Officer's revised schedule of 8 October as Prabhat Khabar (10 October) and ETV Bharat reported it, with claims to 31 October, decisions by 23 November and the final roll on 30 November, so the claims box shows notices being decided until 23 November from 1 November. ev-59 (AAP march in Rohtak) dropped: its source does not name the revision.
