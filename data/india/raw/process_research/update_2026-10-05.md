## 1. Supreme Court, 5 Oct 2026: "Any modified Form 6 has not been approved by us"  [press; order not yet found]
- What: CJI Surya Kant, Bagchi J, V Mohana J, hearing W.P.(Crl.) No. 401/2026, Shailendra Mani Tripathi v.
  Union of India (petition on 14 decisions allegedly taken by the CEC without the other two Commissioners).
  Notice issued, tagged with a similar matter (Rakesh Kumar Singh, mentioned 29 Sept). Interim prayer to
  suspend the CEC not granted.
- Quote (LiveLaw): "Any modified Form 6 has not been approved by us. Let's be clear. When the record is
  produced, we would like to see who has modified," CJI Surya Kant observed.
- Quote (LiveLaw): "The bench ultimately issued notice on the petition, and tagged it with a similar matter
  challenging the ECI decisions."
- Quote (Business Standard): "issued notices to the Election Commission and the Centre"; "If the CEC is
  suspended, the functioning of the Election Commission (EC) will be stalled," the Bench said.
- URLs opened: https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/supreme-court-says-modified-form-6-not-approved-by-it-election-commission-pleas-against-cec-gyanesh-kumar-unilateral-sir-decisions-553100

## 2. ECI letter of 29 Sept: post-SIR "special drive" with the plain statutory Form 6  [press + primary reproduction]
- Quote of the letter (Business Today, citing Indian Express, 2 Oct): "In State/UTs where SIR has already been
  completed, a special drive shall be undertaken by the Chief Electoral Officer, District Election Officers and
  EROs for the enrolment of left out and young/first time eligible electors under the process of continuous
  updation." It reports the drive uses the statutory Form 6 and limits the declaration to the "SIR phase only".
  URL: https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/election-commission-orders-special-drive-in-20-states-where-sir-is-done-plain-statutory-form-6-to-be-used-report-559191-2026-10-02
- Primary reproduction, CEO Uttarakhand press note PN 63/2026 of 03.10.2026: "BLOs shall compare the current
  electoral roll with the pre-SIR electoral roll and identify such electors whose names were there in the
  pre-SIR roll but who are not included in the current electoral roll and prepare a list of such electors."
  and "BLOs will share this list with the BLAs of the recognised political parties."
  URL: https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3cbf8710b43df3f2c1553e649403426df/uploads/2026/10/202610031173939115.pdf
- On site? No. Process page timeline ("forms" and a new timeline entry). The ECI letter itself was not found.
