# Second reading of 30 October 2026: the Commission's revision of 29 October

Every source below was read in full on 30 October 2026 with curl, from the
JSON-LD articleBody where the page carries one and from the page text where it
does not. Times are as the pages print them.

- The News Minute, 30 October 2026, 03:41 UTC
  (https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/sir-deadline-extended-in-andhra-telangana-delhi-and-three-other-states).
  Names all six. Telangana notices to 26 November, Andhra Pradesh to 20
  November, both final rolls 30 November. Maharashtra claims 26 October to 13
  November, notices to 25 November, final 30 November. Delhi claims 30 October
  to 25 November, disposal 30 November to 15 December, final 22 December. "The
  poll panel communicated the revised schedules to the respective Chief
  Electoral Officers following requests from them."
- Business Today, 30 October 2026, 07:46 IST. Same dates for Maharashtra,
  Haryana, Andhra Pradesh and Delhi, final 30 November in the five and 22
  December in Delhi. Says the extension came "two days after" the Supreme
  Court hearing, and "the changes followed requests from the chief electoral
  officers".
- PTI in ThePrint, 29 October 2026, 18:17 UTC. Delhi claims to 25 November,
  disposal by 15 December "according to an Election Commission notification",
  final 22 December. Says the change came "a day after" the Supreme Court
  hearing.
- ANI in the Free Press Journal, 30 October 2026, 10:12 IST. "Official
  communications issued from Nirvachan Sadan on October 29". All six, and
  Nagaland's notice phase "from September 20 to November 25".
- Telangana Today, 30 October 2026, 00:24 IST, and Telangana Today (IANS),
  12:15 IST. Telangana notices to 26 November, earlier 5 November, final 30
  November, earlier 9 November, at the CEO's request. Andhra Pradesh to 20
  November, final 30 November. The notice figures in the notices update.
- Haryana Public Relations, release 10167 of 30 October 2026 (Jhajjar, District
  Election Officer). Hearings on all notices to 20 November 2026, final roll
  30 November 2026 "भारत निर्वाचन आयोग और मुख्य निर्वाचन अधिकारी, हरियाणा के
  निर्देशानुसार", and the draft roll of 31 July 2026.

A primary copy was sought once more. ceodelhi.gov.in,
ceoelection.maharashtra.gov.in, ceotelangana.nic.in and the Commission's site
did not answer from the sandbox, and a web search found no Commission press
note, PIB release or CEO post with the 29 October dates.

## Verdicts

1. **process.json, the six steps.** Verified against the sources above, with
   one correction. Telangana's step started its notice period on 31 July 2026,
   the date the order of 14 May set for the draft. Telangana's draft was
   published on 17 August 2026 (Organiser, 17 August: "The Election Commission
   of India (ECI) on Monday published the draft electoral rolls for all 119
   Assembly constituencies in Telangana"; The South First: "on Monday, 17
   August, released the draft roll"), which is also `draft_date` in
   raw/states.csv. The notice period now starts on 17 August. Andhra Pradesh
   and Haryana start on 31 July (their draft, confirmed for Haryana by the
   Jhajjar release), Maharashtra and Delhi on 31 August, Nagaland on 20
   September, each the state's draft. Maharashtra's label said "letter" and
   Business Today does not, so it reads "Commission's revision". The
   reported_dates entries for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana match their
   sources. The gaps line is right.
   - notes.p3later rewritten so that it says the revision of 29 October is
     drawn, from the reports and the Haryana release in each step's label, and
     that the earlier press dates for Andhra Pradesh and Telangana were
     superseded. Punjab, Jharkhand and Tripura are kept as they were.
   - Timeline entry of 29 October added. The revision came on 29 October, one
     day after the hearing of 28 October, as PTI says. Business Today's "two
     days" counts to its own date of publication, so the entry says "the day
     after". Delhi is a union territory, so the entry says "five states and
     Delhi". What the Court said is taken from court-54 (the Chief Justice,
     as The Indian Express reported, and no order passed).
2. **states.csv Delhi note, normal.json for Nagaland, Haryana and Andhra
   Pradesh, the SOURCES.md files, cases_research/plain.json.** Verified, with
   one correction. Nagaland's note and SOURCES.md said the press reported the
   change on 29 October and named IANS. The report found is ANI in the Free
   Press Journal of 30 October, and the IANS report found (Telangana Today)
   covers Telangana and Andhra Pradesh only. Both now say 30 October and name
   the Free Press Journal.
3. **notices.csv, four rows, and notices/SOURCES.md.** Verified. Every figure
   is in its source: 92,08,443 and 50,690 for Telangana as of 29 October, and
   44,28,858 and 1,73,662 for Andhra Pradesh with no date. The arithmetic in
   the notes holds (44,28,858 less 44,28,444 is 414, and 42,55,630 plus
   1,73,662 is 44,29,292, 434 more than the hearings). "Found ineligible for
   inclusion in the final roll" is what every earlier `deleted_after_notice`
   row counts (Goa, Meghalaya, Karnataka, Telangana), so the category fits.
   One correction. The rows and SOURCES.md said The News Minute gives the
   figures from the Chief Electoral Officer's office. It attributes the notice
   counts to that office ("according to the CEO's office") and the 50,690 to
   the Commission ("The ECI also reported that 50,690 electors had been found
   ineligible"), and now says so.
4. **Page sources.** The two Delhi sentences are right. The three Hyderabad
   sentences put the claims date of the September revision and the dates of
   the October revision in one list after both dates, so they now give each
   revision its own dates.
