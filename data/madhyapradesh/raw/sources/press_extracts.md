# Madhya Pradesh: figures as printed in the press (read 2026-10-06 through Firecrawl)

## Dainik Jagran, 23 Dec 2025, "MP में वोटर लिस्‍ट का ड्राफ्ट जारी, 42.74 लाख मतदाताओं के नाम कटे; 8.65 लाख अनमैप्ड वोटर्स को मिलेगा नोटिस"
https://www.jagran.com/madhya-pradesh/bhopal-mp-voter-list-draft-released-4274-lakh-names-removed-40082485.html
Figures given by the CEO and the Joint CEO at the draft publication:
- 71,930 polling stations; 42,74,160 names not in the draft; draft 5,31,31,983 on 23.12.2025.
- 8,65,832 electors on the draft submitted incomplete forms and will get notices.
- Roll on 06.01.2025: 5,70,92,367. Roll frozen 25.10.2025 for the SIR: 5,74,06,143.
- Draft: men 2,74,82,233; women 2,56,48,831; third gender 919.
- October 2025 -> December 2025 -> fall: men 2,93,91,548 -> 2,74,82,233 -> 19,09,315; women 2,80,13,362 -> 2,56,48,831 -> 23,64,531; third gender 1,233 -> 919 -> 314.
- By reason: dead 8,46,184; absent 8,42,677; permanently shifted 22,78,393; at two or more places 2,76,961; other 29,927.
- 230 EROs, 532 AEROs for claims and objections (ETV Bharat on 21.02.2026 says 533).
- Bhopal (7 ACs): incomplete forms 1,16,925; dead 33,791; absent 1,01,503; shifted 2,86,661; at more than one place 14,171.
- Indore (9 ACs): incomplete 1,33,696; dead 43,741; absent 1,75,424; shifted 1,97,898; more than one place 22,808.
- Jabalpur (8 ACs): incomplete 69,394; dead 51,357; absent 66,678; shifted 1,15,437.
- Gwalior (6 ACs): incomplete 68,540; dead 27,852; absent 61,520; shifted 1,48,273.
