# Tripura sources

**The revision has not reached its draft.** Tripura's SIR was rescheduled by
the Election Commission at the request of political parties, because of the
Village Committee elections of 28 September 2026 in the tribal areas. With
1 January 2027 as the qualifying date, house-to-house enumeration runs
6 November to 5 December 2026, the draft is due 12 December 2026 and the final
roll 15 February 2027. So there is no roll before (frozen), no draft, no names
left off, no notices and no final roll to transcribe, at any level.

Searched on 2026-10-06: the CEO's site (ceo.tripura.gov.in and ceotripura.nic.in
both refused the sandbox, connection reset and proxy 502; not retried further),
one web search limited to the last month for enumeration or roll figures
(nothing for Tripura), and the News On AIR report already cited in
`data/india/raw/states.csv`.
