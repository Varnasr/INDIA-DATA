# Tripura sources

**The revision has not reached its draft.** Tripura's SIR has been put back
twice. The Election Commission first rescheduled it because of the Village
Committee elections of 28 September 2026 in the tribal areas, with enumeration
6 November to 5 December 2026, the draft due 12 December 2026 and the final
roll 15 February 2027. On 29 October 2026, at the Chief Electoral Officer's
request of 19 October, it put the revision back again because elections to 21
urban local bodies are due. Under that letter, as Hindustan Times reported it
on 30 October 2026, house visits run 26 December 2026 to 25 January 2027, the
draft is due 1 February 2027, claims and objections run to 2 March 2027,
notices and claims are to be decided by 1 April 2027 and the final roll is due
7 April 2027. So there is no roll before (frozen), no draft, no names left off,
no notices and no final roll to transcribe, at any level.

Searched on 2026-10-06: the CEO's site (ceo.tripura.gov.in and ceotripura.nic.in
both refused the sandbox, connection reset and proxy 502; not retried further),
one web search limited to the last month for enumeration or roll figures
(nothing for Tripura), and the News On AIR report already cited in
