 Out of the total electors in the Draft Roll, 3,61,159 electors (83.35%) were mapped

with their own/parents’ electoral details of the previous qualifying electoral roll/Last
SIR, as applicable. Whereas, 72135 electors (16.65%) remained unmapped.
Out of 3,61,159 electors in the mapped category, 52,384 electors were identiﬁed as
Logical Discrepancies (LD) by the ECI IT System under various categories.
Total 1,24,519 Notices were issued under the unmapped category and Logical
discrepancies category. After proper hearing of all above cases total 16,485
electors found ineligible and 324 electors have been deleted upon submission of
Form7 due to Death/shifted resulting in a total deletion of 16,809 electors during
claims and objections period .
Hence Total electors who are going to be excluded from Final roll including both
categories who did not submit EF back and found ineligible during hearing totals to
54,209 (37,724 ASDD + 16,485 ineligible)

Decisions in respect of the unmapped electors and cases of Logical Discrepancies
were taken by the concerned Electoral Registration Oﬃcers (EROs)/Assistant
Electoral Registration Oﬃcers (AEROs) after issuing notices and conducting
hearings and/or collecting requisite documents and BLO undertakings, as per the
provisions and facilities made available in ECINET by the Election Commission of
India.
During the Claims and Objections period from 05.07.2026 to 04.08.2026, a total of
2,052 claims for new inclusions were received in Forms6, 16,867 objections were
received in Form7 and 1,549 claims for correction/ shifting were received in Form8.
After due veriﬁcation and disposal of the claims, objections, notices and cases
relating to unmapped and discrepant electors, a total of 1,982 electors were added
and 16,809 electors were deleted from the electoral roll. Out of the 16,809 deleted
electors 16,485 electors were found ineligible during Notice and Hearing period and
deleted.

The net change in the Final electorate is -14,827, representing a change of -3.42%
over the previous Draft roll.
The Final Electoral Roll has been prepared after due consideration of claims,
objections, notices, hearings and veriﬁcation processes prescribed by the Election
Commission of India.
The Final Electoral Roll of SIR 2026 is published on the website of the Oﬃce of the
Chief Electoral Oﬃcer, Sikkim, at www.ceo.sikkim.gov.in on 06.09.2026 and
