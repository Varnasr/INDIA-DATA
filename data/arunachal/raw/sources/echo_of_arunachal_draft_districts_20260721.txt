Itanagar, Jul 21: The Draft Electoral Rolls under the Election Commission of India's Special Intensive Revision (SIR)-2026 were published across Arunachal Pradesh on Tuesday, marking the beginning of the claims and objections period that will continue till August 20 ahead of the final publication of the electoral rolls on September 22.
District Election Offices across the state distributed copies of the Draft Electoral Rolls to recognised political parties and appealed to electors to verify their entries and submit claims for inclusion, correction or deletion of names within the stipulated period.

In Papum Pare district, Deputy Commissioner-cum-District Election Officer Lobsang Tsering released the Draft Electoral Rolls for 13-Itanagar, 14-Doimukh and 15-Sagalee Assembly Constituencies during a coordination meeting with representatives of recognised political parties at Yupia.
The DEO said the Special Intensive Revision had been completed successfully within the timeline prescribed by the Election Commission of India and lauded the efforts of Electoral Registration Officers (EROs), Booth Level Officers (BLOs) and Booth Level Agents (BLAs).
He informed that claims and objections can be filed from July 21 to August 20, while hearings will be conducted from August 21 to September 18.
ERO for 13-Itanagar, Dani Rikang, explained that unlike the routine Annual Summary Revision, the SIR involved door-to-door physical verification of electors to eliminate duplicate, deceased, permanently shifted and other ineligible entries.
According to the revised Draft Electoral Roll, the total electorate in Papum Pare district has come down from 1,02,810 to 53,382, reflecting a reduction of 49,428 electors (48.07 per cent).
Constituency-wise, 13-Itanagar recorded the highest reduction, with the electorate revised from 63,046 to 21,471, followed by 14-Doimukh, where the electorate decreased from 25,077 to 19,038, and 15-Sagalee, where the number of electors was revised from 14,687 to 12,873.
The district now has 24,717 male and 28,665 female electors. Political party representatives assured full cooperation to the District Election Office in ensuring a transparent and error-free final electoral roll.
In Tawang district, the Draft Electoral Rolls for Lumla, Tawang and Mukto Assembly Constituencies were published with a total electorate of 25,512, comprising 12,227 male and 13,285 female voters.
During the revision exercise, Booth Level Officers identified 3,023 electors under categories such as deceased, permanently shifted, absent or untraceable and already enrolled elsewhere. District Election Officer Namgyal Angmo appealed to citizens to verify their electoral details and submit claims or objections before August 20.
In Tirap district, the Draft Electoral Rolls for all four Assembly Constituencies were published after 34,177 Enumeration Forms were collected and digitised out of 40,041 registered electors. The remaining 5,864 electors were categorised as uncollectable due to migration, death, prolonged absence, untraceability and other valid reasons.
The District Election Office advised electors requiring verification of their own or ancestral records to attend hearings with relevant documents before preparation of the Final Electoral Roll.
In Lohit district, Deputy Commissioner-cum-DEO Kesang Ngurup Damo released the Draft Electoral Roll for 44-Tezu (ST) Assembly Constituency, comprising 16,828 general electors, including 8,255 male and 8,573 female voters. With 254 service voters, the total electorate stands at 17,082.
The District Election Office, Upper Subansiri, announced that post-publication activities have commenced following publication of the Draft Electoral Roll. Electors have been asked to submit Form 6 for inclusion, Form 7 for deletion or objections and Form 8 for corrections either through their BLOs or online using the ECINET App.
In West Siang district, the Draft Electoral Rolls for 27-Liromoba (ST), 30-Along West (ST) and 31-Along East (ST) Assembly Constituencies were published. Out of 42,546 electors, 36,254 Enumeration Forms were digitised during the SIR exercise. Officials identified 3,992 permanently shifted, 1,243 deceased, 974 absent or untraceable, 173 duplicate enrolments and 110 other cases.
The District Election Office clarified that genuine electors whose names are absent from the Draft Electoral Roll can apply for inclusion during the claims and objections period. Electors enrolled at multiple locations will be retained only at one place after due verification.
The Election Commission's Special Intensive Revision aims to ensure inclusion of all eligible electors while removing ineligible entries through extensive field verification, public participation and scrutiny by recognised political parties.
Across the state, District Election Officers have urged eligible voters to inspect the Draft Electoral Rolls, verify their particulars and submit applications in the prescribed forms before August 20.
The Final Electoral Rolls are scheduled to be published on September 22, 2026, after disposal of all claims and objections. (With inputs from DIPROs)
