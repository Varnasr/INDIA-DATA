YUPIA, July 21: The Office of the District Election Officer
(DEO), Papum Pare, on Tuesday convened a coordination meeting with
representatives of recognised political parties at the Deputy Commissioner’s
Conference Hall, Yupia, following the draft publication of the Electoral
Roll-2026.
 Deputy Commissioner-cum-District Election Officer (DEO), Shri
Lobsang Tsering, officially released the Draft Electoral Rolls for 13-Itanagar,
14-Doimukh and 15-Sagalee Assembly Constituencies and handed over copies of the
draft electoral rolls to the representatives of the recognised political
parties.
 Addressing the meeting, Shri Tsering highlighted the successful
completion of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR)-2026 within the timeline
prescribed by the Election Commission of India (ECI). He elaborated on the
extensive efforts undertaken by the District Election Office in carrying out
the revision exercise and congratulated the Booth Level Officers (BLOs), Booth
Level Agents (BLAs) and the Electoral Registration Officers (EROs) for their
dedication and unwavering commitment in successfully completing the exercise.
 The DEO informed that the period for filing claims and
objections has commenced from July 21, 2026, and will continue till August 20,
2026. He further stated that hearings on the claims and objections will be
conducted from August 21 to September 18, 2026, as per the schedule notified by
the Election Commission of India.
 Appealing for active participation, Shri Tsering urged all
recognised political parties to extend  their wholehearted
cooperation during the claims and objections period and the subsequent hearings
to facilitate the preparation of a transparent, inclusive and error-free final
electoral roll.
 Electoral Registration Officer (ERO), 13-Itanagar Assembly
Constituency, Smt. Dani Rikang, made a detailed PowerPoint presentation on the
outcome of the recently concluded Special Intensive Revision (SIR)-2026 in
Papum Pare district.
 She explained that the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) is
distinct from the routine Annual Summary Revision. Unlike the annual exercise,
which primarily processes applications received online or through designated
facilitation centres, the SIR involves door-to-door physical verification of
electors by Booth Level Officers (BLOs) across the Assembly Constituencies. She
stated that the objective of the exercise is to improve the accuracy and
integrity of the electoral roll by identifying and removing ineligible entries,
including duplicate registrations, deceased electors whose deaths remain
unreported, permanently shifted voters and other non-genuine or “ghost”
electors.
 Presenting the statistical outcome of the recently concluded
SIR-2026, Smt. Rikang informed that the total number of electors in Papum Pare
district has been revised from 1,02,810 in the pre-revision electoral roll to
53,382 in the Draft Electoral Roll, reflecting a reduction of 49,428 electors
(48.07%).
 She further informed that, based on the recently concluded
SIR-2026, the electorate in 13-Itanagar Assembly Constituency has been revised
from 63,046 to 21,471, registering a reduction of 41,575 electors (65.94%).
Similarly, 14-Doimukh Assembly Constituency has been revised from 25,077 to
19,038, a reduction of 6,039 electors (24.08%), while 15-Sagalee Assembly Constituency
has been revised from 14,687 to 12,873, reflecting a reduction of 1,814
electors (12.35%).
 She also shared the gender-wise composition of the Draft
Electoral Roll prepared following the recently concluded SIR-2026, stating that
the district now has 24,717 male and 28,665 female electors. Constituency-wise,
13-Itanagar has 9,451 male and 12,020 female electors; 14-Doimukh has 8,914
male and 10,124 female electors; while 15-Sagalee has 6,352 male and 6,521
female electors.
