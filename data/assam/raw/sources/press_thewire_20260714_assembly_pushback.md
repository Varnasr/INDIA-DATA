193 People Declared 'Foreigners' by Assam's Tribunals 'Pushed Back' Into Bangladesh in Last Two Years, Says State Govt

The data shows that 67 declared foreigners have been sent to Bangladesh through “expulsion,” while the 126 other declared foreigners who have been listed as “repatriated” have been categorised as those who have been “sent back.”

Over the last two years, 193 people who were declared foreigners by Assam’s Foreigner Tribunals have been “pushed into Bangladesh, data submitted to the state assembly on Monday (June 13) by the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) government in Assam has revealed, reported

The Assam government has stated in its response that between July 1 2024 and June 30 2026, 1,679 people have been sent to Bangladesh. The people who were pushed back have been categorised as those who have been sent through formal deportation, those who have been “sent back” and those who have been “expelled”.

As per the data, a total of 193 people declared foreigners by Assam’s Foreigner Tribunals have been “pushed” into Bangladesh over the last two years, including 67 ‘declared foreigners’ who have been sent across the border by invoking 1950 Immigrants Expulsion Act revived last year, data submitted by the Assam government in the legislative assembly shows.

AIUDF’s Ajmal had also asked that how many of the “deported persons” have appealed in the high court and Supreme Court.

“No identified illegal immigrant is repatriated if any appeal is pending in Honourable High Court or Supreme Court of India. There is no information available if any repatriated people appealed before the Honourable High Court or Supreme Court of India,” states the government’s reply.
