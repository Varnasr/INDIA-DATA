Over 2.4L names deleted in Assam voter list revision - The Tribune

Around 2.43 lakh voters’ names have been deleted from the final electoral rolls of Assam following the special revision (SR) exercise, according to details released by the Election Commission on Tuesday. This has resulted in a net decline of 0.97 per cent in the total number of voters compared with the draft list published in December 2025.

Incidentally, Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma’s own Jalukbari constituency recorded the deletion of over 4,300 names from a total of 2.11 lakh voters. In Jalukbari, 2,06,314 electors figure in the final list — a decline of 4,310 voters, or 2.05 per cent, compared with 2,10,624 listed in the draft roll published on December 27 last year, according to a press release issued by Kamrup Metropolitan district authorities.

“The total number of voters in the draft list was 2,52,01,624, which has come down by 2,43,485 voters to 2,49,58,139 in the final voters list, a 0.97 per cent decrease compared to the draft voter list,” a statement issued by the office of Assam’s Chief Electoral Officer (CEO) said.

The final voter list comprises 1,24,82,213 male voters, 1,24,75,583 female voters and 343 from the third gender.

The SR process carried out in Assam differs from the special intensive revision (SIR) undertaken in several other states, as the final list of the National Register of Citizens (NRC), unique to Assam and intended to identify illegal immigrants, has not yet been notified.

The SR exercise did not require physical verification of documents by booth-level officers (BLOs), with the focus instead on submission of forms by the public for inclusion of new voters, objections to entries, or corrections.

In the first phase of the exercise, BLOs conducted house-to-house surveys across 126 Assembly constituencies between November 22 and December 20, 2025. The draft electoral roll was published on December 27, followed by the claims and objections process.

On December 27 last year, election officials said 4,78,992 names had been identified for deletion due to deaths, 5,23,680 due to shifting from registered locations, and 53,619 due to multiple entries — a total of 10,56,291 names.

The statement said appeals regarding non-inclusion, corrections in names or addresses may be filed before the district magistrate within 15 days of publication of the final voter list. A second appeal may be made before the CEO within 30 days.

The SR process came under scrutiny during the claims and objections phase, with Opposition parties alleging that large-scale objections were filed to facilitate mass deletions. Several parties, including the Congress, submitted complaints to the Election Commission last month seeking a detailed probe into the process and an audit of all Form 7 applications filed over the previous three months across constituencies.
