Assam still has 91,385 D-voters on electoral rolls: CM Sarma tells House

Foreigners' Tribunals declared 56,728 D-voters foreigners, while 31,789 have already been expelled from Assam, govt reveals

had been removed from Assam's electoral rolls during the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) exercise, Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma, on Monday, said that 91,385 "Doubtful-voters" continue to exist in the state's electoral rolls.

Replying to a question by Congress MLA Nurul Islam during the Budget Session of the Assam Legislative Assembly, Sarma said Sonitpur district accounts for the highest number of D-voters at 13,719, followed by Barpeta with 8,081, while Udalguri and Nagaon each have over 7,800 such voters.

He further said the process of marking individuals as D-voters has been in force in Assam since 1997, in accordance with Election Commission directives, for persons whose citizenship credentials are found to be doubtful or disputed.

Responding in his capacity as Minister in charge of the Home and Political Department, Sarma said that of the D-voter cases adjudicated so far, 56,728 persons have been declared foreigners by Foreigners' Tribunals (FTs).

He added that 831 individuals were subsequently declared foreigners by courts after approaching the Gauhati High Court.

At the same time, the tribunals have declared 65,171 D-voters as Indian citizens, while the Gauhati High Court and the Supreme Court have recognised 42 and three individuals, respectively, as Indian citizens, he said.

Replying to another question raised by Congress MLA Wazed Ali Choudhury, Sarma said the number of D-voters in Assam stood at 1,99,596 when the category was first introduced in 1997.

He informed the House that 2,44,144 D-voter cases have been referred to Foreigners' Tribunals so far. Of these, 2,05,659 cases have been disposed of, resulting in 56,728 persons being declared foreigners.

The Chief Minister further stated that apart from D-voter references, the Assam Border Police has forwarded 1,90,657 other suspected foreigner cases to Foreigners' Tribunals. Of these, 1,55,490 cases have been disposed of, with 1,15,945 persons declared foreigners.

Of the total persons declared foreigners through the tribunal process, 31,789 have been expelled, Sarma added.

Introduced by the Election Commission in 1997, the D-voter category is unique to Assam and applies to individuals whose citizenship status is under question.

Such voters are barred from exercising their franchise until their citizenship claims are settled by Foreigners' Tribunals and subsequent judicial processes.

The issue of D-voters is one of the most contentious topics in Assam's political and social sphere. Several elections have been fought over the issue of illegal Bangladeshi immigrants, who are initially marked as D-voters if their names are found in the electoral rolls.

Deletion or regularisation of a D-voter is done as per orders of the Foreigners Tribunals and the subsequent judgements by higher courts.
