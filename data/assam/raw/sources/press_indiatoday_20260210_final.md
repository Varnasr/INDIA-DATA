2.4 lakh names dropped from Assam voter rolls after EC's special revision

According to the Chief Electoral Officer (CEO), Assam, the final voter list reflects a decrease of 2,43,485 voters from the draft list, which had recorded 2,52,01,624 voters.

The Election Commission said the final voter list has been prepared after the completion of the verification process and disposal of claims and objections. (Photo: ITG)

The Election Commission on Tuesday published the final voter list for the

Special Revision (SR) 2026 covering all 126 Assembly constituencies

As per the gender-wise breakup in the final list, Assam has 1,24,82,213 male voters, 1,24,75,583 female voters, and 343 voters in the third gender category.

The integrated draft voter list for Special Revision 2026 was published on December 27, 2025. Prior to this, a house-to-house verification drive was conducted across the state between November 22 and December 20, 2025.

The period for filing claims and objections remained open from December 27, 2025, to January 22, 2026.

The Election Commission said the final voter list has been prepared after the completion of the verification process and disposal of claims and objections.

The Special Revision is considered a middle ground between the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) and the Special Summary Revision (SSR). It is less intensive than the ongoing SIR process being carried out across several states.
