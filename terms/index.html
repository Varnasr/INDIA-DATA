Terms and privacy

Who publishes this site

This site is published by PinPoint Ventures, a development research and evaluation practice. Write to info@pinpointventures.in about anything on it.

What the figures are

Every figure here is counted from lists and tables published by the Election Commission of India and the Chief Electoral Officers of the states, or from other public sources named on each method page. The site has no connection with the Election Commission, any Chief Electoral Officer, any government or any political party.

The counts are checked against the totals printed in their sources, and each method page says what the figures can show and what they cannot. They can still contain errors. Before relying on a figure, check it against the source the method page names. Nothing here is legal advice.

Corrections and grievances

If a figure is wrong, a source is misread, or anything on the site concerns you, write to info@pinpointventures.in. We acknowledge every message within 24 hours and reply in substance within 15 days. A correction is made on the page itself.

Privacy

No name, EPIC number or address of any elector appears anywhere on this site. There are no accounts, no forms and no advertising, and the site sets no cookies.

The site counts visits without knowing who makes them. When a page opens, or a chart is shared or downloaded, or the site is installed or searched, it adds one to a tally for that day, that kind of action and that page. Nothing else is sent: no IP address, no device identifier, no search words and nothing that could tie a count to a person. The tallies are held at Supabase in Mumbai. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is counted at all.

Your browser keeps three preferences on your own device, the language, the colour theme and the reading font, and, if you install the site or come back to it, copies of the pages you have read so that they open without a connection. None of this is sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

Some files come from other services, which receive the ordinary request any web page makes, your IP address included, under their own policies. The pages are served by Netlify and GitHub Pages, the charts' library by cdnjs (Cloudflare), and the fonts for Indian languages by Google Fonts. Sharing on WhatsApp opens WhatsApp, and what happens there is governed by WhatsApp.
