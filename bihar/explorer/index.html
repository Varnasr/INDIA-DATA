        Removals come from the deletion lists of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR), published by the Chief Electoral Officer, Bihar. Phase 1 was published with the draft roll of 1 August 2025, and phase 2 before the final roll. Booth figures cover phase 1 only, because only phase 1 was listed by booth. Constituency figures include both phases. Removals per 100 electors are the names removed divided by the constituency's electors on the 2025 roll. A winning margin is the winner's votes less the runner-up's, from the declared 2025 Assembly results. The NDA here is the BJP, JD(U), LJP(RV), HAM(S) and RLM, and INDIA is the RJD, INC, the Left parties, VIP and IIP. Only totals are published, never names or EPIC numbers. FAQ · Method.
