        Removals come from the Chief Electoral Officer's SIR deletion lists: phase 1 with the draft roll of 1 August 2025, and phase 2 before the final roll. Booth figures cover phase 1 only, because only phase 1 was listed by booth. Constituency figures include both phases. Removals per 100 electors are divided by the constituency's 2025 electorate, and margins are the declared 2025 Assembly results. The NDA here is the BJP, JD(U), LJP(RV), HAM(S) and RLM, and INDIA is the RJD, INC, the Left parties, VIP and IIP. Only totals are published, never names or EPIC numbers. FAQ · Method.
