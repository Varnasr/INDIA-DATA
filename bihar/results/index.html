Deletions and results

The revision removed more names than the winning margin in most of Bihar's constituencies, including most of the seats the NDA took from INDIA. This page sets the removals against the 2025 margins first, then against the alliances and parties that held each seat.

1. Removals against the 2025 margin

Names removed in each seat set against the margin by which the seat was won in 2025.

Each reason for removal, on its own, against the margin

The phase 1 list records the reason the officer entered against every name. Here each reason is taken alone: the number of seats where the names removed for that one reason exceed the 2025 margin, by the alliance that won.

2. Seats, grouped by who held them

Deletions per 100 electors, with each seat labelled by the 2020 Assembly winner, the 2024 Lok Sabha segment leader, and the 2025 Assembly winner.

3. Booths within the same seat

Comparing whole seats mixes politics with geography. Comparing booths inside one constituency does not: the registration officer, the villages and the pattern of migration are the same on both sides. Here the booths the NDA led in 2024 are set against the booths INDIA led, seat by seat.

NDA-led minus INDIA-led booths, seat by seat

Mean names removed per booth, NDA-led booths minus INDIA-led booths, in each constituency with at least 20 of each. Above zero means NDA-led booths lost more.
