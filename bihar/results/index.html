Deletions and results

Did the revision fall harder on one side's seats? The answer depends on how the question is asked, so this page asks it four ways: by which alliance held each seat in three elections, by booths within the same seat, by party, and by whether deletions exceed the margin a seat was won by.

2. Booths within the same seat

Across seats, politics and geography move together: Seemanchal loses more names and votes differently. Inside one constituency the geography is shared, so booths the NDA led in 2024 can be set against booths INDIA led, seat by seat.

NDA-led minus INDIA-led booths, seat by seat

Mean names removed per booth, NDA-led booths minus INDIA-led booths, in each constituency with at least 20 of each. Above zero means NDA-led booths lost more.

A booth's 2024 leader tracks who lives there: caste, religion, and how many work away from home. The within-seat gap is a description of where names were removed, and cannot say why.

5. Deletions against the 2025 margin

In how many seats did the revision remove more names than the 2025 winner's margin of victory?

This is a scale comparison, not a claim that any result would have differed. The deleted include the dead, those enrolled twice and those who have moved away, and no one knows how the rest would have voted.

2024 segment leaders are summed from booth-level Form 20 votes. In nine seats the extract's party labels were wrong and have been corrected (LJP(RV) appeared as "BSP", HAM(S) as "JD(S)", and so on); after correction the top two parties in all 40 seats match the declared winner and runner-up, at shares within a point of the published ones.
