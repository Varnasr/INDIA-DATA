What was corrected

1. Every booth was counted about six times

The booth file carries one row per booth for each of its top three 2024 parties; the religion file one row per booth for each sex. Joining the two and summing gave each booth's deletions roughly six times over: 39.2 million, which the dashboard labelled "2020" and set against the true 6.56 million as a decline of 83%. There is no 2020 deletion series in these files. The column name "deletion by Booth in Jan 2020" refers to the January 2025 roll that the SIR lists are keyed to. Found by noting that the "2020" total divided by the true total was 5.98, and that the source had 464,881 booth rows against 77,837 booths: 5.97.

2. Party labels in the 2024 extract were wrong in nine seats

Vote shares summed from booths match the declared results to a tenth of a point; the party names attached to them do not. Each correction below is shown with its evidence. After correction, the top two parties in all 40 seats are the declared winner and runner-up.

3. "Devi" was classified as a caste

The surname mapping filed Devi under Lohar (EBC). Devi is a title, not a surname, and the commonest entry among the removed, so two million women were counted as EBC and the caste chart showed EBC at 60.9%. Titles and given names are now set aside as carrying no caste information, and caste is read from guardians' surnames first.

4. The district and constituency caste figures were the state survey, repeated

Every district in district_caste_block_shares.csv carried EBC 36.01%, BC 27.12%, SC 19.65%, ST 1.68%, General 15.52%: the 2023 survey's state shares, multiplied by each district's deletions. They said nothing about who was removed there. They are no longer used.

5. Smaller things

Minapur (90) is a Vaishali segment, not Muzaffarpur, correcting the public constituency table. The 2020 labels in the booth file are the 2020 Assembly winners, not Lok Sabha as the column header said. Kalyanpur (16, 131) and Pipra (17, 42) are joined by number, never by name.

The research questions, answered again

The earlier dashboard posed fourteen questions. Each is restated, with the earlier answer where it changed, and the answer the corrected data gives.

Definitions. Removed: named on the SIR deletion lists (absent, shifted, dead or enrolled twice); the lists in these files do not carry the reason. Per 100 electors: removed across both phases, divided by the constituency's 2025 electorate. Segment leader: the party with most votes in the assembly segment, summed from booths. Booth leader: the party with most votes in the booth. Nothing on these pages identifies an individual.
