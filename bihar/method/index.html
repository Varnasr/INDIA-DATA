How the lists were prepared

One count per booth

The booth list carries each booth once for each of its three leading parties in 2024, with the same removal count on every row. Each booth is counted once, which gives 77,837 booths and 65,59,626 phase 1 removals, the Chief Electoral Officer's total.

Party names matched to the declared result

Vote counts summed from the booths match the declared 2024 result in every Lok Sabha seat. In nine seats the party name attached to a column of votes differed from the declared result, and is matched to it, with the evidence for each below. After matching, the first and second parties in all 40 seats are the declared winner and runner-up.

Titles are not surnames

Devi, Kumari and Khatun are carried by most women in place of a surname and say nothing about caste. They are set aside, and caste is read first from the surname of the father or husband.

Constituencies are joined by number

Bihar has two Kalyanpurs (16 and 131) and two Pipras (17 and 42), so every join uses the constituency number. Minapur (90) belongs to the Vaishali Lok Sabha seat.

Border constituencies

A constituency counts as a border constituency when its district touches Nepal, West Bengal, Jharkhand or Uttar Pradesh.

Research questions

What this shows and what it does not

The lists show how many names were removed, from which booths, and who the removed were by sex and by name, and they allow the removals to be set against the margins that decided each seat. They do not show why an individual name was removed, whether the removal was right, or how the elector voted; religion and caste are read from names and are estimates. Those questions can be settled only by a name-by-name verification, starting with the constituencies where the removals exceed the winning margin. Questions and answers.

Removed: named on the SIR deletion lists. Per 100 electors: removals across both phases divided by the constituency's 2025 electorate. Booth leader: the party with most votes in the booth in 2024. Segment leader: the party with most votes in the assembly segment in 2024.
