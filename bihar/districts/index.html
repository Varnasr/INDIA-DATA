    What moves with the deletion rate

    Each panel sets the district deletion rate against one characteristic. r is the linear correlation and ρ the rank correlation, and both run from −1 to 1.

    All factors together

    With only 38 districts these factors cannot be told apart. The poorest districts are also the least urban and least literate, and in the north-east they send the most migrants out. Read each panel as a description of the districts, not as a cause.

    Every district

    Density and urban share: Census 2011, the last count at district level. Women's literacy and marriage before 18: National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5), district fact sheets, fielded in Bihar in 2019-20. Per-capita district domestic product: Bihar Economic Survey, 2021-22 at constant prices. Electors per booth: the 2025 electorate over the number of booths with SIR lists.
