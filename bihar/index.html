Bihar SIR 2025: who was dropped from the draft roll

The Special Intensive Revision removed 65.6 lakh names from Bihar's roll in its first phase and 3.5 lakh more in the second. These pages follow those removals down to the polling booth and set them against the 2020, 2024 and 2025 results.

Deletions and the three elections

The same deletions, grouped by which alliance held each seat. The comparison depends on which election labels the seat, so all three are shown.

This is an association across constituencies, not evidence of targeting. Deletions are highest in Seemanchal and the border belt, where out-migration is heaviest and where the "Other" seats (Purnia's independent MP, AIMIM) sit. The results page compares booths within the same constituency, which holds geography fixed.
