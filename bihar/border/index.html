Bihar 2025

Figures as of 30 September 2025, when the final roll was published.

Border

    Deletions per 100 electors, by border

    Names removed for every 100 electors on the 2025 roll, with constituencies grouped by the border their district touches. Seats on two borders form their own group.

    Share of all removals

    Each group's share of Bihar's removals beside its share of the electorate. Where the first bar is longer, the group lost more names than its size would suggest.

    Who was removed

    Women's share of removals, and the range for the share of names classified Muslim. Religion is guessed from names. The low end leaves out unclassified names and the high end counts all of them as Muslim.

    Phase 2 against phase 1

    Phase 1 removed names before the draft roll of 1 August 2025, and phase 2 before the final roll. The chart gives phase 2 as a share of phase 1, so a higher share means more names came off after the draft.

    Every border constituency

    Click a column to sort.

    The Nepal and Bengal belts, and Seemanchal within them, also send the most migrants out of Bihar, for a season or for good. A migrant who has moved away is rightly marked "shifted". A higher rate on the border fits that explanation as well as any other. The lists show that a name came off the roll, and cannot show which explanation is right.

    A constituency counts as a border seat when its district touches Nepal, West Bengal, Jharkhand or Uttar Pradesh. The shares of Muslim names come from the name classification in the booth files. The low figure counts only names classified Muslim, and the high figure adds every name left unclassified.
