The border belts

Bihar borders Nepal to the north, West Bengal and Jharkhand to the east and south, and Uttar Pradesh to the west. The revision's stated purpose included removing those not entitled to be on the roll, so the border constituencies were watched closely. Here they are against the interior.

The Nepal and Bengal belts, and Seemanchal within them, are also where seasonal and long-term out-migration is heaviest, and a migrant who has moved is a legitimate "shifted" deletion. A higher border rate is consistent with that as much as with anything else; these data record the removal, not its reason.

Border classification: a constituency counts as a border constituency when its district touches Nepal, West Bengal, Jharkhand or Uttar Pradesh. Muslim-name shares come from the name classification in the booth files; the low end counts only names classified Muslim, the high end adds every unclassified name.
