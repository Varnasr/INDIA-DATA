    Every border constituency

    Click a column to sort.

    The Nepal and Bengal belts, and Seemanchal within them, also send the most migrants out of Bihar, seasonally and for good, and a migrant who has moved away is rightly marked "shifted". A higher rate on the border fits that explanation as well as any other. The data record that a name was removed, and nothing about why.

    A constituency counts as a border seat when its district touches Nepal, West Bengal, Jharkhand or Uttar Pradesh. Muslim-name shares come from the name classification in the booth files. The low figure counts only names classified Muslim, and the high figure adds every name left unclassified.
