    Religion

    The booth files classify each removed elector's name as Hindu, Muslim or unclassified. An unclassified name could be either, so the Muslim share is given as a range, from counting none of them as Muslim to counting all of them.

    Muslim share of removals against the 2011 census, by district

    The bar is the range from name classification. The dark dot is the district's Muslim share of the population in 2011, and the open dot is that share carried forward to 2025. Where a dot sits inside the bar, the removals match the population.

    The 2011 census is the most recent count of religion by district. The 2025 figures carry each district's 2011 share forward at the growth rates of its Muslim and total populations between the 2001 and 2011 censuses. Jehanabad and Arwal were one district in 2001, so both take their joint rate.

    Caste

    The lists give a surname for each removed elector and for the father or husband named beside them. The father's or husband's surname tells more, because most women are listed as Devi, Kumari or Khatun, which say nothing about caste. Surnames are classified in two ways. Classification A uses the project's mapping of surnames to castes. Classification B is strict and counts only surnames that belong almost entirely to one group, setting aside names such as Singh, Prasad, Ray, Mahato, Mandal, Sharma, Thakur, Chaudhary and Manjhi, which several groups carry. Only findings that hold under both are reported.

    The 25 commonest surnames among the removed

    Counting the same surnames on the whole draft roll would turn each surname's share of the removed into a removal rate. Surname counts from the draft roll, even for a sample of booths, would give each caste group its own removal rate, as age and religion have in the Hyderabad analysis.

    The caste baseline is the Bihar caste-based survey released on 2 October 2023. It counted 13,07,25,310 people, of whom 36.01% were EBC, 27.12% BC, 19.65% SC, 1.68% ST and 15.52% unreserved, with Muslims at 17.70%. Muslim castes listed under EBC (10.29 points), BC (2.09) and unreserved (4.80) are moved to the Muslim group here, which leaves the Hindu castes at EBC 25.72, BC 25.03 and General 10.72. The strict classification gives a surname to a group only if almost everyone carrying it belongs to that group.
