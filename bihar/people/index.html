Women, religion, caste

The roll records each elector's sex. It does not record religion or caste, so this page reads those from names: the removed elector's own name, and the surname of the father or husband listed beside it. Each estimate is set against a population baseline, the 2011 census for sex and religion and the 2023 Bihar caste survey for caste.

Religion

The booth files classify each removed elector's name as Hindu, Muslim, or unclassified. The unclassified could fall either way, so the Muslim share is a range: from counting none of them to counting all of them.

Muslim share of removals against the 2011 census, by district

The bar spans the range from name classification; the dot is the district's Muslim share of population in 2011. Where the dot sits inside the bar, the removals are consistent with the population.

The 2011 census is the latest with religion by district. Bihar's Muslim share rose from 16.87% then to 17.70% in the 2023 caste survey.

Caste

The lists give a surname for each removed elector and for the father or husband named beside them. The guardian's surname carries more: most women are listed as Devi, Kumari or Khatun, which say nothing about caste. Surnames are classified two ways. Classification A uses the project's surname-to-caste mapping. Classification B is strict: it counts only surnames that belong almost entirely to one group, and sets aside names such as Singh, Prasad, Ray, Mahato, Mandal, Sharma, Thakur, Chaudhary and Manjhi that are carried across groups. A finding that holds under both is one to rely on.

The 25 commonest surnames among the removed

A surname's share among the removed becomes a removal rate once the same surname is counted on the whole draft roll. Draft-roll surname counts, even for a sample of booths, would give each caste group its own removal rate, as age and religion have in the Hyderabad analysis.

Caste survey baseline: the Bihar caste-based survey released on 2 October 2023 (population 13,07,25,310: EBC 36.01%, BC 27.12%, SC 19.65%, ST 1.68%, unreserved 15.52%; Muslims 17.70%). Muslim castes listed within EBC (10.29 points), BC (2.09) and unreserved (4.80) are moved to the Muslim group, so the Hindu-caste shares are EBC 25.72, BC 25.03 and General 10.72. The strict classification assigns a surname to a group only where it belongs almost entirely to that group.
