Hyderabad Old City SIR 2026: who was struck off

Every elector on the pre-revision rolls of Charminar, Yakutpura, Malakpet, Chandrayangutta and Bahadurpura, sorted into three fates: struck off, kept but summoned to a hearing, or untouched. Because the files cover every elector, these pages can give true rates for every group, and compare groups inside the same polling part.

What the Old City files show

Religion is estimated from names; the roll records none. Old City wards carry large migrant populations, so genuine shifting sits alongside any erroneous deletion, and these data describe what the revision did, not why. The count is from the September exports; the Commission extended the schedule on 7 September, so final figures will differ.
