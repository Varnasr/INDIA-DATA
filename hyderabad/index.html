Hyderabad Old City SIR 2026: who was struck off

The Special Intensive Revision reached Hyderabad in 2026. These pages follow every elector on the pre-revision rolls of five Old City constituencies, Charminar, Yakutpura, Malakpet, Chandrayangutta and Bahadurpura, to one of three outcomes: struck off, kept but summoned to a hearing, or left on the roll.
