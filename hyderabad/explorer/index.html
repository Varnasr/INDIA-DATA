      Deleted means on the Absent-Shifted-Dead list and missing from the draft roll, the roll published after the removals, in the September 2026 exports. The Commission extended the schedule on 7 September, as Telangana Today reported, with claims to 7 October, hearings to 5 November and the final roll on 9 November, so the count will change. Malakpet is read by polling-station name because its export has no part numbers. "At hearing" counts the Election Commission's discrepancy list and the no-mapping list together. The no-mapping list holds electors who could not be traced to the last intensive revision, and the discrepancy list holds mismatches in names, ages and family links. Ages come from the draft and ASD files. Religion is guessed from names, and a separate classification agrees on 99% of them.
