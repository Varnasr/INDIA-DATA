    Every rate, within-part comparison and correlation on these pages is computed from the part-level counts shown in the explorer. No name, EPIC number or address is published.

    Definitions

    Deletion rate

    Electors on the Absent-Shifted-Dead list divided by electors on the roll before the revision, for the group in question. The files cover every elector, so no sample is involved and each rate is exact for its group.

    Within-part difference

    For two groups, the rate of one minus the rate of the other inside each polling part, pooled across parts and weighted by the smaller group in each part. Parts with fewer than 20 of either group are left out. This holds the neighbourhood, the booth-level officer and the housing constant.

    Part correlations

    A rank correlation runs from -1 to 1 and measures how closely the parts' order on one measure follows their order on another. These are Spearman rank correlations across parts with at least 100 electors, within each constituency. The untraceable share uses parts with at least 20 deletions.

    Summoned

    Kept on the draft roll but listed for a hearing, from the ECI discrepancy list and the no-mapping list together. One elector can be flagged under more than one rule, so the rule counts add up to more than the number of people summoned.

    What this shows and what it does not

    The lists show how many electors were struck off and from which parts, with each person's age and sex, a religion guessed from the name, and whether they link to the 2002 roll. They cannot show whether any single removal was right. Questions and answers.
