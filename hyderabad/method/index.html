Telangana 2026

Figures as of 17 August 2026, when the draft roll was published. The final roll is due on 30 November 2026.

Method

    Sources

    WhatSourceUsed for
    Pre-revision roll, Absent-Shifted-Dead (ASD) list, discrepancy and no-mapping listsThe Chief Electoral Officer (CEO), Telangana, SIR lists for the five constituencies, compiled elector by elector and aggregated to the polling part, September 2026Each elector's outcome, age band, sex, reason code and 2002 link, added up by polling part
    ReligionGuessed from each elector's name, because the roll does not record religion. A separate classification agrees on 99% of namesHindu-name and Muslim-name rates
    Hearing schedulesThe same lists, 28 August to 12 OctoberRules, slots, daily load
    2023 Assembly resultECI, Telangana general election 2023Valid votes and margins

    Telangana's dates, as set by the order of 14 May 2026, are drawn beside every other state's on How the revision was run.

    Every rate, within-part comparison and correlation on these pages comes from the part-level counts shown in the explorer. No name, EPIC number or address is published.

    Definitions

    Exclusion rate

    Electors on the Absent-Shifted-Dead list divided by electors on the roll before the revision, for the group in question. The lists cover every elector, so no sample is involved and each rate is exact for its group.

    Within-part difference

    For two groups, the rate of one minus the rate of the other inside each polling part, pooled across parts and weighted by the smaller group in each part. Parts with fewer than 20 of either group are left out. This holds the neighbourhood, the booth-level officer and the housing constant.

    Part correlations

    A rank correlation runs from -1 to 1 and measures how closely the parts' order on one measure follows their order on another. These are Spearman rank correlations across parts with at least 100 electors, within each constituency. The untraceable share uses parts with at least 20 exclusions.

    Summoned

    Kept on the draft roll but listed for a hearing, from the ECI discrepancy list and the no-mapping list together. One elector can be flagged under more than one rule, so the rule counts add up to more than the number of people summoned.

    What this shows and what it does not

    The lists show how many electors were left off the draft and from which parts, with each person's age and sex, a religion guessed from the name, and whether they link to the 2002 roll. They cannot show whether any single exclusion was right. Questions and answers.
