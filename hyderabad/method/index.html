Every rate, within-part comparison and correlation on these pages is computed from the part-level counts shown in the explorer. No name, EPIC number or address is published.

Definitions

Deletion rate

Electors on the Absent-Shifted-Dead list divided by electors on the pre-revision roll, for the group in question. Because the files cover every elector, this is a true rate for each group. In Bihar the files cover only the removed, so the Bihar pages give shares of removals and rates per 100 electors on the 2025 roll instead.

Within-part difference

For two groups, the rate of one minus the rate of the other inside each polling part, pooled across parts with each part weighted by its smaller group. Parts with fewer than 20 of either group are left out. This holds the locality, the booth-level officer and the housing stock fixed, and is the Hyderabad counterpart of the within-seat comparison in Bihar.

Part correlations

Spearman rank correlations across parts with at least 100 electors, within each constituency. The untraceable share uses parts with at least 20 deletions.

Summoned

Kept on the draft roll but listed for a hearing: the ECI discrepancy list plus the no-mapping list. One elector can carry more than one rule, so rule counts sum to more than the people summoned.

What this shows and what it does not

The lists show how many electors were struck off, from which parts, and who they were by age, sex, religion estimated from name, and 2002 linkage. They do not show whether any individual removal was right. That needs verification before the final roll: every removal entered as "untraceable or absent", every removal of an elector with no link to the 2002 roll, and every part that lost far more than the rest of its constituency.
