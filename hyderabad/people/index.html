Age, sex, religion and the 2002 roll

The Old City lists cover every elector on the pre-revision roll, so each group has its own rate: the share of that group struck off. Groups live side by side in the same polling parts, so each gap can also be measured inside a part, where the booth officer, the street and the housing are the same for everyone.

The lists give religion and age separately, not together, so part of a religion gap may be an age gap. Religion is estimated from names.
