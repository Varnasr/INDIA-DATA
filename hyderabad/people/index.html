    The same gap, inside each part

    A polling part is the smallest section of the roll. This is the Hindu-name rate minus the Muslim-name rate inside each part, averaged across parts with at least 20 of each group and weighted by the smaller group. Comparing inside a part holds the neighbourhood constant.

    Religion is guessed from names. The lists give religion and age separately and never together, so if one group is older, part of a gap by religion may be a gap by age.

    The 2002 roll

    Linked and unlinked to 2002

    The revision asks each elector to link to the roll of 2002, the last intensive revision, through their own name or a parent's. The chart gives the deletion rate for electors with that link and for those without. Malakpet's list does not record the link.
