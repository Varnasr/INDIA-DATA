Parts

    How part rates are spread

    A polling part is the smallest section of the roll. Parts with at least 100 electors, grouped by the share struck off.

    Range within each constituency

    The lowest and highest part rate, and the rates a quarter, half and three quarters of the way up the list of parts (Q1, the median and Q3).

    Muslim share of a part against its deletion rate

    Each dot is a polling part. Parts with more Muslim-name electors lost a smaller share of their roll.

    What moves with the part rate

    A rank correlation, from -1 to 1, measures how closely the parts' order on one measure follows their order by removal rate. A negative value means the rate falls as the measure rises. Each is worked out across the parts of one constituency.

    Parts that stand apart

    Parts whose removal rate is furthest above or below the typical (median) part in their own constituency.

    Parts with fewer than 100 electors are left out of the rates, because one household can move them by several points. A part's Muslim share is the share of its roll before the revision whose names are classified Muslim. "Untraceable" is the share of a part's deletions given the Election Commission's reason code Untraceable/Absent.
